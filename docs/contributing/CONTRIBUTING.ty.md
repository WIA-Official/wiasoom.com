## Te Ture o te Noa

Ua fa'avae matou i te fa'aturi i te mau huru e te mau huru i te mau taata atoa.

- **E mea to'eto'e.** A fa'aro'o i te mau taata i te mana.
- **E mea fa'avae.** A fa'ata'ita'i i te mau fa'ata'iraa e mea faufaa, eiaha e fa'ata'ita'i i te mau fa'ata'iraa e mea fa'ahiahia.
- **E mea fa'aro'o.** E tauturu matou i te 254 reo e e fa'aro'o i te mau haapiiraa mai te mau fenua atoa i te ao.
- **Eiaha e fa'ahiahia.** E mea zero te fa'ahiahia i te mau huru e te mau fa'ahiahia.

## 🔌 E nafea e fa'ata'ita'i i te plugin

Ua i ai te WIA SOOM i te mau huru plugin e faufaa — e taea e oe te hanga i to oe plugin i roto i te 5 miniti.

### Te Haapiiraa Tere
```bash

./scripts/soom-create-plugin.sh
```
### Te Haapiiraa Tūtohu

E a'o i te **[Plugin Developer Guide](docs/PLUGIN_DEVELOPER_GUIDE.md)** no:
- Te mau fa'ata'ita'iraa API atoa
- Te mau hi'oraa e te mau fa'ata'ita'iraa

- Te mau huru e te mau ture ha'apapū

### E fa'ata'ita'i i to oe plugin

1. E fa'aa'ite i te [Plugin Store](https://wiasoom.com)
2. E fa'aa'ite i to oe plugin i roto i `plugins/{your-plugin-name}/`
3. E fa'ata'ita'i i te Pull Request
4. I muri i te a'o, e haere to oe plugin i roto i te Plugin Store no te mau taata atoa!

## 🌐 Te mau haapiiraa faata'ita'iraa (254 reo)

E tauturu te WIA SOOM i te **254 reo** — mai i te Amharic i te Zulu, e te mau reo Braille e te mau reo RTL.

### E nafea e fa'ata'ita'i i te mau fa'ata'ita'iraa

- Te fa'ata'ita'iraa i te reo: `src/renderer/src/i18n/en.json`
- E i ai te mau fa'ata'ita'iraa 254 i roto i te vahi e tahi
- E fa'ata'ita'i i te mau fa'ata'ita'iraa i roto i `scripts/translate-patch.js` (GPT-4o-mini API)

### E nafea e fa'ata'ita'i i te mau fa'ata'ita'iraa

#### Ohipa 1: E fa'ata'ita'i i te fa'ata'ita'iraa i te reo

1. E hi'o i te fa'ata'ita'iraa i te reo: `src/renderer/src/i18n/{lang-code}.json`
2. E fa'ata'ita'i i te fa'ata'ita'iraa e te fa'ata'ita'iraa
3. E fa'ata'ita'i i te PR i te fa'ata'ita'iraa

#### Ohipa 2: E fa'ata'ita'i i te mau fa'ata'ita'iraa e te mau fa'ata'ita'iraa
```bash
# Sync all languages with new English keys
npm run i18n:sync

# Translate only new/missing keys
node scripts/translate-patch.js
```
#### Ohipa 3: E hi'o i te mau fa'ata'ita'iraa i te mau fa'ata'ita'iraa

E i ai te mau fa'ata'ita'iraa 254 i te mau fa'ata'ita'iraa. E mea faufaa te mau hi'oraa i te mau fa'ata'ita'iraa!

1. E hi'o i to oe fa'ata'ita'iraa
2. E hi'o i te mau fa'ata'ita'iraa
3. E fa'ata'ita'i i te mau fa'ata'ita'iraa e te mau fa'ata'ita'iraa
4. E fa'ata'ita'i i te PR

### Te mau kooti reo

E whakamahi matou i te mau kooti ISO 639-1 (e.g., `ko`, `en`, `ja`, `ar`, `hi`) e te mau fa'ata'ita'iraa i te mau vahi e te mau fa'ata'ita'iraa (e.g., `zh-CN`, `pt-BR`).
