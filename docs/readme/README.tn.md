## 🔥 What's Never Been Done Before

### 1. Image, File & Video Upload in SSH Terminal — World's First

Go na le CLI client mo Lefatsheng le le letlhagelang go go fa monyetla go dira se. Re se dirile.

Tlhopho ya setshwantsho sa phoso mo SSH terminal ya gago. Dira faele ya log. Dira video ya go palelwa go diragatsa. AI e e bonang, e e sekaseka, mme e go bolelela se se phoso — detlhakgolo mo session ya terminal.

- **Ctrl+Shift+V** — tsenya setshwantsho ka kotlolo mo terminal
- **Drag & Drop** — tsenya ditshwantsho, faele, video mo terminal
- **Video keyframe extraction** — ffmpeg e sekaseka video ka faele ka faele
- **All 3 AI providers** — GPT-4o Vision, Gemini Vision, Claude Vision

Se se amanang le se ke se se sa kgethegeng mo go le faele ya terminal — kae kae.

### 2. AI Arena — Multi-Agent Battle

2 go ya go 4 AIs di rarabolola bothata bo bo amanang ka nako e e amanang — e se ka go buisana fela, empa go edit code mo server ya gago. AI nngwe le nngwe e sebetsa mo Git branch e e ikgethegileng. Bontsha diphetho di le mmalwa. Kgetha mo go atlegang. Tse dingwe di a kgethegwa.

Cross-review: AI nngwe le nngwe e fa diphetho mo tharabololeng ya AI e nngwe.

Go na le sesupo se sengwe se se dirang se.

### 3. AI Self-Healing Agent

CPU ya server ya gago e phakgamala. Disk e tletse. Tshebeletso e senngwa ka 3 AM. O robetse.

AI agent ya WIA SOOM e lemoga bothata, e sekaseka ntlha ya ntlha, e bopa leano la go lokisa, e kopa tumelo ya gago (gape e ka diragatsa ka bo eona fa o e setse o e dirile), mme e rarabolola bothata. 5-level autonomous loop — go simolola go lebelela go ya go lokisa ka botlalo.
