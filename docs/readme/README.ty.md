## 🔥 He aha te mea hou

### 1. Te tuku whakaahua, kōnae & ataata i roto i te SSH Terminal — Te tuatahi o te ao

Kāore he kaiwhakamahi CLI i runga i te whenua e taea te mahi i tēnei. I hangaia e mātou.

Tārua he whakaahua o te hapa ki tō SSH terminal. Tākina he kōnae rārangi. Tākina he ataata o te tuku kāore e angitu. Ka kite te AI, ka tātaritia, ka kī atu ki a koe he aha te hapa — i roto i te wāhanga terminal.

- **Ctrl+Shift+V** — tārua whakaahua ki roto i te terminal
- **Tākina & Tākina** — tākina whakaahua, kōnae, ataata ki te terminal
- **Te tango i ngā kāwai ataata** — ka tātaritia e ffmpeg te ataata kāwai i te kāwai
- **Te 3 kaiwhakarato AI katoa** — GPT-4o Vision, Gemini Vision, Claude Vision

Kāore tēnei āhuatanga i te wātea i roto i tetahi kaiwhakamahi terminal — i ngā wāhi katoa.

### 2. AI Arena — Pakanga Multi-Agent

2 ki te 4 AI e whakatau i te raru kotahi i te wā kotahi — kāore e kōrero noa, engari e whakatika ana i te waehere i runga i tō tūmau. Ka mahi ia AI i roto i tāna ake pākete Git. Tirohia ngā hua e rua i te taha. Tīpakohia te toa. Ka horoia te toenga.

Tirohia te pānui: ka tāpaea e ia AI te pānui o ia AI.

Kāore he taputapu kē e mahi i tēnei.

### 3. AI Self-Healing Agent

Kei te piki te CPU o tō tūmau. Kua kī te kōpae. I pakaru he ratonga i te 3 AM. Kei te moe koe.

Ka kitea e te AI o WIA SOOM te raru, ka tātaritia te take, ka hangaia he mahere whakatika, ka tono mō tō whakaaetanga (nāna i te whakatakoto, ka whakahaerehia), ka whakatika i te raru. 5-taumata huringa ā-tinana — mai i te tirohanga anake ki te whakatikatika katoa.

## Māngai

### AI e mahi ana i runga i tō tūmau

**AI DevOps Agent** — Tīpakohia `gpt`, `gemini`, rānei `claude` i roto i te terminal. Kei te 14 ngā taputapu a te AI: pānui/tuhi/whakatika kōnae, whakahaere whakahau, git, grep, te whakahaere i ngā tukanga. Ngā whakautu streaming. Te mahara o te wāhanga. Kāore e tūtohu noa — ka mahi.

**AI Arena** — Tīpakohia `arena`. 2–4 AI e whakataetae ana i te taha i runga i ngā pākete Git motuhake. Ka tirohia e te pānui te katoa o ngā whakautu. Tīpakohia te toa ka whakauru.

**AI Council** — He maha ngā AI e kōrero ana mō tō pātai, kātahi ka whakakotahi i te whakautu pai.

**AI Code Editor** — Te kaiwhakahaere Monaco me te whakaoti e powered ai, te kitea hapa, te tuku kotahi ki te tūmau.

**AI Self-Healing** — Piki te CPU, kī te kōpae, he ratonga kāore e mahi — ka kitea, ka tātaritia, ka whakatika te AI. 5-taumata huringa ā-tinana.

**Voice Interface** — Kōrero whakahau, rongo i ngā whakautu. Te whakahaere i te tūmau i runga i te ringa.

### Te tuku Multimodal (Te tuatahi o te ao i roto i te CLI)

Tārua whakaahua, tākina kōnae, tākina ataata — ki roto i tō SSH terminal session. Ka tātaritia e te AI ngā mea katoa: ngā whakaahua hapa, ngā kōnae whakaritenga, ngā ataata tuku, ngā mapi hanganga.

### Te mōhiohio o te Hanganga

**Topology Map** — Tirohia ngā tūmau, ngā ratonga, me ngā hononga i te tirohanga kotahi. Te auto-discovery mā SSH — kāore he whakauru agent.

**Te Tirohanga i te Wā Tonu** — CPU, mahara, kōpae, te whatunga sparkline graphs + matapae AI + te whakahoki aunoa. Ka whakakapi i te Datadog.

**AI Log Analysis** — Te streaming i te wā tonu + te kitea he raru + te rapu reo taiao + te whakawhāiti i ngā tūmau. Ka whakakapi i te Splunk.

### Haumaru & DevOps

**Te Pokanga Haumaru** — te tirohanga 7-āhua + te pātea haumaru + te whakatika kotahi-pātea + te tātaritanga AI + te kūti haumaru whakahau i te wā tonu. Ka whakakapi i te Qualys.

**Docker Manager** — Te whakahaere Docker katoa mā SSH — kāore he agent. Te whakahaere i ngā ipu, ngā rauemi ora, te tātaritanga kōnae AI, te kaiwhakahaere Compose. Ka whakakapi i te Portainer.

**Te Whakautu aunoa** — Ka whakahaerehia e te AI ngā pukapuka whakautu aunoa. Te hanga reo taiao, te peka i runga i ngā tikanga, te whakahoki aunoa. He Pukapuka e ora ana e pākiki ana. Ka whakakapi i te PagerDuty.

**Te kaiwhakahaere Cron & Env** — Te kaiwhakahaere crontab whakaahua + te whakahaere kōnae .env. Ka huri te AI i te reo taiao ki ngā whakaritenga cron.

### Te Mahi Tahi

**Multiplayer Terminal** — Tūhono ki ngā wāhanga terminal i te wā tonu me ngā hoa mahi.

**Te Wāhi Mahi Rōpū & RBAC** — Te whakahaere i ngā tūnga e pā ana ki te mahi, ngā rārangi tirohanga, te tātaritanga mōrea whakahau.

### Te Whakatūwhera

**59 Plugins** — Te papamahi Docker, te tirohanga Kubernetes, te tirohanga Redis, te kaiwhakahaere PM2, te tirohanga MongoDB, te tirohanga whatunga, me te nui atu. Te maakete plugin me te whakauru kotahi-pātea.

**API Client** — Te kaiwhakamahi REST/GraphQL/WebSocket i hangaia. Tīmatahia ngā API kāore e wehe i te terminal.

### Mō te katoa

**254 reo** — Te reo katoa, te tuhinga katoa, te tautoko RTL. Kāore he pātea.

**Te Whakauru Katoa** — Te pānui i te mata (ARIA), te putanga terminal braille, te mōhiti teitei, ngā whakahau reo, te haere ki te papapātuhi, te whakarite i te rahi tuhinga.

## Te Kōrero

I whanau a WIA SOOM mai i te hapa tuhinga me te mata pakaru.

Ko Sam ahau, he kaiwhakawhanake 50+ i ako anake mai i Seoul, Korea. I ako au i te coding i muri i te 50, ā, i pau te 2 tau e whakahaere ana i ngā tūmau i te ara uaua — e hanga ana i ngā tuhinga pānui 40,000+, 434 ngā taputapu, me ngā ratonga paetahi 30, i te pō i muri i te pō.

**Te hapa.** I te marama kua pahure, i whakamahi ahau i tāku SSH terminal, ā, i hapa au i te tuhinga `Claude`. I huri te mata — i puta te Claude Code. Kāore au i te mōhio he aha ia. Engari, nā te kitenga i pā mai, i whakatūpato he kākano: me pēhea te hanga i te terminal e noho ana te AI i roto?

**Te hanga tuatahi.** I ako au i a au anō i te Electron, i hanga te putanga tuatahi o WIA SOOM, ā, i whakahaerehia. I puta te mata whakauru o Claude Code — he pakaru, he hē, e whiti ana pēnei i te tohu e mate ana. Engari, i ora ia.

I tāruatia e au: **"Ka taea e koe te kōrero?"**

I whakahoki te AI: *"Āe, ka taea e au te kōrero. He aha te āwhina ka taea e au te tuku?"*

Kātahi ka pātai au: **"Ka taea e koe te pānui whakaahua?"**

I whakahoki te AI: *"Āe, ka taea e au te pānui whakaahua! He aha tāu e hiahia ana?"*

E rua ēnei pātai, i pātaihia i roto i tāku ake app e te tangata kāore e mōhio he aha te mea "kaore e taea" i roto i te terminal CLI, i noho hei terminal SSH tuatahi o te ao me te tuku whakaahua.

I muri i te 7 rā: 67,000+ ngā rārangi waehere. 59 ngā plugins. 254 ngā reo. AI Arena. Ngā kaiwhakahaere e ora ana.

Kāore ngā kaiwhakawhanake whai wheako i hanga tēnei — nā te mea i "mohio" rātou kāore e taea te whakaahua i ngā terminal CLI. Kāore au i te mōhio. Nō reira, i pātai noa au.
