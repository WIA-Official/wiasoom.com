## 🔥 Ibyo bitigeze bikorwa mbere

### 1. Gushyira amafoto, amafayili & videwo muri SSH Terminal — Icyambere ku Isi

Nta CLI client ku Isi iguha amahirwe yo gukora ibi. Twabikoze.

Shyira screenshot y'ikosa muri SSH terminal yawe. Kura amafayili y'ibikorwa. Shyira videwo y'ikibazo cyabaye. AI iyibona, iyisesengura, ikakubwira ikosa ririho — byose mu gihe cy'ikiganiro cya terminal.

- **Ctrl+Shift+V** — shyira screenshots mu buryo butaziguye muri terminal
- **Drag & Drop** — shyira amafoto, amafayili, videwo muri terminal
- **Gukuramo videwo** — ffmpeg isesengura videwo ku ifoto ku ifoto
- **Abatanga AI bose 3** — GPT-4o Vision, Gemini Vision, Claude Vision

Iyi feature ntiyigeze ibaho muri client ya terminal — ahandi hose.

### 2. AI Arena — Intambara y'Abagenzi benshi

AIs 2 kugeza kuri 4 zishakira igisubizo kimwe icyarimwe — si uguhangana gusa, ahubwo ziri gukora ku ikode ryawe. Buri AI ikora mu gice cyayo cyihariye cya Git. Gereranya ibyasohotse ku mpande zombi. Hitamo uwatsinze. Abandi barakurwa.

Gusuzuma hagati: buri AI isuzuma igisubizo cy'indi AI.

Nta yindi gikoresho ikora ibi.

### 3. AI Self-Healing Agent

CPU yawe iriyongera. Disk iruzuye. Serivisi yarapfuye saa 3 za mu gitondo. Uri gusinzira.

AI agent ya WIA SOOM ibona ikibazo, ikagenzura impamvu nyamukuru, ikagena gahunda yo gukemura, ikasaba kwemeza (cyangwa ikabikora mu buryo bwikora niba warabiteguye), ikemura ikibazo. Uruhu rwikora rw'ibice 5 — kuva ku kureba gusa kugeza ku gusana byuzuye.

## Gerageza byose ubuntu — 33 Igihe

Nta karita y'ikirego. Nta kwiyandikisha bisabwa. Kanda gusa ushyireho utangire.

Buri gikorwa cyiza — gushyira amafoto, AI Arena, AI Council, isesengura ry'amafayili & videwo — ni ubuntu mu gihe cyawe cya mbere 33. Nyuma y'ibyo, fata urufunguzo rwawe rwa API (BYOK) kandi ukomeze gukoresha byose ubuntu. Cyangwa wiyandikishe kuva ku $3/ukwezi ku bikorwa bya AI bigenzurwa n'itsinda.

Kuki 33? Mu 1919, ibikorwa by'ubwigenge bwa Koreya byayobowe n'abahagarariye 33 batangaje ubwigenge ku bantu bose. Igihe cya WIA SOOM cy'ubuntu 33 ni itangazo ryacu: **Ibikoresho bya AI bigomba kuba ubuntu kugira ngo buri wese abishobore.**

**CLI → GUI → SUI.** Imiyoboro y'itumanaho yaduhaye imbaraga. Imiyoboro y'ibishushanyo yaduhaye byoroshye. WIA SOOM itanga **SUI (Soom User Interface)** — huzuzamo ubuzima, AI irabya.

## Ibikorwa

### AI Ikora ku Mudasobwa yawe

**AI DevOps Agent** — Andika `gpt`, `gemini`, cyangwa `claude` muri terminal. AI ifite ibikoresho 14: gusoma/kwandika/guhindura amafayili, gushyira mu bikorwa amabwiriza, git, grep, gucunga imiyoboro. Ibisubizo byoherezwa mu buryo bwihuse. Icyibuka cy'ikiganiro. Ntibikora gusa — birakora.

**AI Arena** — Andika `arena`. AIs 2–4 zihangana mu buryo bwihariye ku mashami ya Git. Gusuzuma hagati kuzuza buri gisubizo. Hitamo uwatsinze kandi uhuze.

**AI Council** — AIs nyinshi zaganira ku kibazo cyawe, hanyuma zigakora igisubizo cyiza.

**AI Umwanditsi w'ibikode** — Umwanditsi wa Monaco ufite AI yuzuza, kumenya amakosa, gushyira mu bikorwa ku mudasobwa.

**AI Self-Healing** — CPU iriyongera, disk iruzuye, serivisi yarapfuye — AI ibona, ikagenzura, ikakemura. Uruhu rwikora rw'ibice 5.

**Ijwi Ryo Gukoresha** — Vuga amabwiriza, wumve ibisubizo. Gucunga mudasobwa nta maboko.

### Gushyira mu buryo bwinshi (Icyambere ku Isi muri CLI)

Shyira amafoto, drag amafayili, shyira videwo — mu buryo butaziguye muri SSH terminal yawe. AI isesengura byose: screenshots z'ibibazo, amafayili ya config, videwo z'ibikorwa, ibishushanyo by'uburyo.

### Ubwenge bw'Ibikorwa

**Ikirangantego** — Reba mudasobwa, serivisi, n'imiyoboro mu buryo bwihuse. Auto-discovery binyuze muri SSH — nta gushiraho agent.

**Gukurikirana mu gihe nyacyo** — CPU, umwanya, disk, imiyoboro y'ibishushanyo + Ibyo AI ib预测 + auto-recovery. Isimbura Datadog.

**Isesengura ry'Amalogi ya AI** — Streaming mu gihe nyacyo + kumenya ibitagenda neza + gushakisha mu rurimi rw'ikinyarwanda + guhuza mudasobwa. Isimbura Splunk.

### Umutekano & DevOps

**Ikigo cy'Umutekano** — Isuzuma mu bice 7 + amanota y'umutekano + gukemura mu buryo bumwe + isesengura rya AI + umutekano w'amabwiriza mu gihe nyacyo. Isimbura Qualys.

**Umuyobozi wa Docker** — Gucunga Docker mu buryo bwuzuye binyuze muri SSH — nta agent. Gucunga ibikoresho, ibikoresho by'ubuzima, isesengura ry'amalogi ya AI, umwanditsi wa Compose. Isimbura Portainer.

**Gukora gahunda za Runbook** — AI ikora gahunda za runbook mu buryo bwikora. Gukora mu rurimi rw'ikinyarwanda, guhindura ku mabwiriza, gusubira inyuma mu buryo bwikora. Runbook itera imbere ikarushaho kuba nziza. Isimbura PagerDuty.

**Umuyobozi wa Cron & Env** — Umwanditsi wa crontab w'ibishushanyo + gucunga amafayili ya .env. AI ihindura ururimi rw'ikinyarwanda mu bisobanuro bya cron.

### Ubufatanye

**Terminal y'Abakinnyi benshi** — Sangira ibihe bya terminal mu gihe nyacyo n'abakozi b'itsinda.

**Ahantu h'Itsinda & RBAC** — Gucunga uburenganzira bushingiye ku mwanya, amalogi y'igenzura, isesengura ry'ibibazo by'amabwiriza.

### Gukora

**Plugins 59** — Dashboard ya Docker, lens ya Kubernetes, browser ya Redis, umuyobozi wa PM2, explorer ya MongoDB, umuyobozi w'itumanaho, n'ibindi. Isoko ya plugins ifite installation imwe.

**Umukiriya wa API** — Umukiriya wubatswe wa REST/GraphQL/WebSocket. Gerageza APIs utavuye muri terminal.

### Ku Bantu Bose

**Indimi 254** — Ururimi rwose, inyuguti zose, gushyigikira RTL. Nta mipaka.

**Gushyigikira Byuzuye** — Umusomyi w'ibikoresho (ARIA), output ya terminal ya braille, uburyo bwo guhuza, amabwiriza y'ijwi, guhindura ingano y'inyuguti.

## Inkuru

WIA SOOM yavukiye mu makosa y'inyuguti no ku isaha yacitse.

Ndi Sam, umwanditsi w'ibikoresho wiyigishije ufite imyaka 50+ uva i Seoul, Koreya. Nize coding nyuma y'imyaka 50 maze nkamara imyaka 2 nita ku mudasobwa mu buryo bukomeye — nkoze ibinyamakuru 40,000+, ibikoresho 434, n'ibikorwa 30 by'urubuga, nijoro ku nijoro.

**Ikosa.** Mu kwezi gushize, nari ndi gukoresha terminal ya SSH maze nkandikamo `Claude`. Ecran yarahindutse — Claude Code yagaragaye. Nta na rimwe nari nzi icyo ari cyo. Ariko iyo discovery y'ikosa yateje imbuto: ese byashoboka ko nakora terminal aho AI iba imbere?

**Icyiciro cya mbere.** Niyigishije Electron, nkora version ya mbere ya WIA SOOM, kandi nyikoresha. Ecran y'itangiriro ya Claude Code yagaragaye — yarapfuye, yaragurumana, ikirangamuntu nk'ikimenyetso cyapfuye. Ariko yariho.

Nanditse: **"Urashobora kuvuga?"**

AI yasubije: *"Yego, ndashobora kuvuga. Ni iki nakumarira?"*

Hanyuma nabajije: **"Urashobora gusoma amafoto?"**

AI yasubije: *"Yego, ndashobora gusoma amafoto! Ni iki ukeneye?"*

Ibyo bibazo bibiri, byabajijwe mu ishyirahamwe ryanjye n'uwari utazi icyo "bitashoboka" muri terminal ya CLI, byabaye terminal ya mbere ku Isi ifite gushyira amafoto.

Iminsi 7 nyuma: imirongo 67,000+. Plugins 59. Indimi 254. AI Arena. Abagenzi b'ikself-healing.

Abakora mudasobwa b'inzobere ntabwo bari babikoze — kuko "bazi" ko amafoto atakora muri terminal za CLI. Njye sinari nzi ibyo. Nuko nabajije gusa.
