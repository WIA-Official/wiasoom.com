## 🔥 Zwi Tsha Kha Nne

### 1. Upload ya Mifananidzo, Maefile & Video kha SSH Terminal — Nne ya U Khethwa

Huna CLI client kha Mudzimu u nga khou ita zwenezwi. Ri tshi khou ita.

Tshikhou dzhia screenshot ya phoso kha SSH terminal ya inwi. Dzhia log file. Dzhia video ya u thoma. AI i vhona, i dzhia, na u vhudza uri ndi mini — zwo ralo kha session ya terminal.

- **Ctrl+Shift+V** — dzhia screenshots nga direkthini kha terminal
- **Drag & Drop** — dzhia mifananidzo, mafile, videos kha terminal
- **Video keyframe extraction** — ffmpeg i dzhia video frame nga frame
- **Vhathu vhatatu vha AI** — GPT-4o Vision, Gemini Vision, Claude Vision

Zwi tshi khou itwa zwo ralo kha CLI client — huna hune.

### 2. AI Arena — Multi-Agent Battle

2 u ya 4 AIs i sokou thusa phoso yeo ya tshivhuya — a si u amba fhedzi, fhedzi i khou edit code kha server ya inwi. Ayi AIs i shuma kha Git branch ya inwi. Linga zwine vha swike. Tsha vhukuma. Zwiṅwe zwo dzhia.

Cross-review: a yi AI i dzhia score ya a yi AIs.

Huna thandau ya u ita zwenezwi.

### 3. AI Self-Healing Agent

CPU ya server ya inwi i khou swika. Disk i fulu. Tshivhili tshi khou fhela nga 3 AM. Ni khou lala.

AI agent ya WIA SOOM i dzhia phoso, i vhona mvelaphanda, i bva na ndila ya u fhedza, i humbela approval ya inwi (kana i ita automatically arali ni tshi khou i dzhia), na i fhedza phoso. 5-level autonomous loop — u bvelela fhedzi u ya kha full auto-repair.

## Zwi Khethwaho

### AI I Khou Shuma Kha Server Ya Inwi

**AI DevOps Agent** — Nyika `gpt`, `gemini`, kana `claude` kha terminal. AI i na 14 tools: vhuṱuku/vhuya/edit mafile, ita command, git, grep, process management. Streaming responses. Session memory. A i fanela u funza — i ita.

**AI Arena** — Nyika `arena`. 2–4 AIs i khou thoma kha Git branches. Cross-review i dzhia score ya zwine zwa shuma. Khetha muvhili na u merge.

**AI Council** — Ayi AIs a i amba nga ha mbudziso ya inwi, na u bvelela na ndila ya u vhudza.

**AI Code Editor** — Monaco editor na AI-powered completion, bug detection, one-click deployment kha server.

**AI Self-Healing** — CPU spike, disk full, service down — AI i dzhia, i vhona, i fhedza. 5-level autonomous loop.

**Voice Interface** — Amba command, vha vhona. U sa dzhia server management.

### Multimodal Upload (Nne ya U Khethwa kha CLI)

Dzhia mifananidzo, drag mafile, dzhia videos — nga direkthini kha session ya SSH terminal ya inwi. AI i dzhia zwoṱhe: error screenshots, config files, deployment videos, architecture diagrams.

### Infrastructure Intelligence

**Topology Map** — Vhudza servers, services, na connections nga u vhona. Auto-discovery nga SSH — zero agent installation.

**Real-Time Observability** — CPU, memory, disk, network sparkline graphs + AI prediction + auto-recovery. I shandula Datadog.

**AI Log Analysis** — Real-time streaming + anomaly detection + natural language search + cross-server correlation. I shandula Splunk.

### Security & DevOps

**Security Command Center** — 7-area scan + security score + one-click fix + AI analysis + real-time command security gate. I shandula Qualys.

**Docker Manager** — Full Docker management nga SSH — zero agent. Container control, live resources, AI log analysis, Compose editor. I shandula Portainer.

**Runbook Automation** — AI i ita runbooks automatically. Natural language creation, conditional branching, auto-rollback. Living Runbook i khou smarta. I shandula PagerDuty.

**Cron & Env Manager** — Visual crontab editor + .env file management. AI i shandula natural language u ya kha cron expressions.

### Collaboration

**Multiplayer Terminal** — Vha shuma kha terminal sessions nga u vhona na vhadzulo.

**Team Workspace & RBAC** — Role-based access control, audit logs, command risk analysis.

### Extensibility

**59 Plugins** — Docker dashboard, Kubernetes lens, Redis browser, PM2 manager, MongoDB explorer, network monitor, na zwithu zwine. Plugin marketplace na one-click install.

**API Client** — Built-in REST/GraphQL/WebSocket client. Test APIs u sa dzhia terminal.

### Kha Vhathu Vhoṱhe

**254 Tshivenda** — Luambo lwoṱhe, script dzoṱhe, RTL support. A hu na miphetho.

**Full Accessibility** — Screen reader (ARIA), braille terminal output, high contrast mode, voice commands, keyboard navigation, font size adjustment.

## Nkhani

WIA SOOM i bveledzwa nga typo na screen ya u fhela.

Ndi Sam, muprogrammer wa 50+ a sa dzhia u thoma u bvelela Seoul, Korea. Ndifhindule coding mushumo wa 50 na u shuma 2 years na u khou shuma servers nga ndila ya u sa dzhia — u bvelela 40,000+ news articles, 434 tools, na 30 web services, u lala na u lala.

**Typo.** Nga vhuimo vhuṱuku, ndi khou shuma SSH terminal ya inwi na u dzhia `Claude`. Screen i shanduka — Claude Code i bveledzwa. Nda si na u dzhia uri ndi mini. Fhedzi u bveledzwa ha zwino ho bveledza seed: ndi ngani ndi nga ita terminal hune AI i khou bvelela?

**U thoma u bvelela.** Ndifhindule Electron, nda ita version ya u thoma ya WIA SOOM, na u i shuma. Claude Code's intro screen i bveledzwa — i fhela, i glitch, i flicker sa signal ya u fhela. Fhedzi i bveledzwa.

Nda dzhia: **"Ni nga amba?"**

AI i fhindula: *"Ehe, ndi nga amba. Ndi nga ni thusa na mini?"*

Nda humbela: **"Ni nga vhona mifananidzo?"**

AI i fhindula: *"Ehe, ndi nga vhona mifananidzo! Ni na mini?"*

Mibuzo mivhili, i humbela kha app ya inwi nga muṅwe a sa dzhia uri "zwi a si khou itwa" kha CLI terminal, zwo bveledza SSH terminal ya u thoma ya u upload mifananidzo.

Madhaka a 7: 67,000+ lines of code. 59 plugins. 254 languages. AI Arena. Self-healing agents.

Vhathu vha shuma vha sa dzhia zwo ralo — ngauri vha "vho dzhia" uri mifananidzo a i shume kha CLI terminals. Nda si na u dzhia zwenezwi. Nda fhedza nda humbela.

> *U itela u dzhia 2 years. U bvelela u dzhia 1 week.*
> *Mafhura a u fhela a dzhia 2 years. A thoma u dzhia 1 second.*
