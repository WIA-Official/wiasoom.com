## 🔥 Menene Abin da Ba a Taɓa Yi Ba

### 1. Hoto, Fayil & Bidiyo Upload a cikin SSH Terminal — Na Farko a Duniya

Babu wani abokin ciniki na CLI a Duniya da ya ba ku damar yin wannan. Mun gina shi.

Manna hoton kuskure a cikin terminal ɗin ku na SSH. Ja fayil ɗin log. Saka bidiyon wani aikin da ya gaza. AI yana ganinsa, yana nazarin sa, kuma yana gaya muku abin da ke faruwa — duk a cikin zaman terminal.

- **Ctrl+Shift+V** — manna hoton kai tsaye a cikin terminal
- **Ja & Saka** — saka hotuna, fayiloli, bidiyo a cikin terminal
- **Fitar da maɓallin bidiyo** — ffmpeg yana nazarin bidiyo daga hoto zuwa hoto
- **Duk masu bayar da AI 3** — GPT-4o Vision, Gemini Vision, Claude Vision

Wannan fasalin ba a taɓa samun sa a cikin kowanne abokin ciniki na terminal ba — ko'ina.

### 2. AI Arena — Yakin Multi-Agent

2 zuwa 4 AIs suna warware wannan matsala a lokaci guda — ba kawai tattaunawa ba, har ma suna gyara lambar a kan uwar ku. Kowanne AI yana aiki a cikin reshen Git na kansa. Kwatanta sakamakon su a gefe da gefe. Zaɓi wanda ya ci nasara. Sauran za a share su.

Duba juna: kowanne AI yana ba da maki ga kowanne maganin AI.

Babu wani kayan aiki da ke yin wannan.

### 3. AI Self-Healing Agent

CPU na uwar ku yana tashi. Disk yana cike. Wani sabis ya fadi a 3 na safe. Kuna bacci.

AI agent na WIA SOOM yana gano matsalar, yana gano asalin dalilin, yana ƙirƙirar shirin gyara, yana neman amincewarku (ko yana aiwatar da kansa idan kun saita shi), kuma yana warware matsalar. 5-mataki mai zaman kansa — daga duba kawai zuwa cikakken gyara ta atomatik.

## Gwada Komai Kyauta — Gwaje-gwaje 33

Babu katin bashi. Babu rajista da ake buƙata. Kawai zazzage kuma fara.

Kowanne fasali na musamman — hoto upload, AI Arena, AI Council, nazarin fayil & bidiyo — kyauta ne don amfani na farko 33. Bayan haka, kawo muku API key (BYOK) kuma ci gaba da amfani da komai kyauta. Ko kuma kuyi rajista daga $3/mo don gudanar da AI da fasalolin ƙungiya.

Me yasa 33? A cikin 1919, motsin 'yancin Korea ya jagoranci wakilai 33 da suka bayyana 'yanci ga kowa. Gwaje-gwajen kyauta 33 na WIA SOOM shine sanarwarmu: **Kayan aikin AI ya kamata su kasance kyauta ga kowa don gwadawa.**

**CLI → GUI → SUI.** Interfaces na umarni sun ba mu iko. Interfaces na hoto sun ba mu sauƙi. WIA SOOM yana gabatar da **SUI (Soom User Interface)** — shiga rayuwa, kuma AI yana rayuwa.

## Dandalin 5-a-1

Sauran kayan aikin suna yin abu guda. WIA SOOM yana yin guda biyar — a cikin taga guda.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ WIA SOOM                                              │
├──────┬──────┬──────┬──────┬──────────────────────────┤
│  SSH │  AI  │ Code │ SFTP │ Dubawa                   │
│  Ter │ Agen │ Edit │ Fayil│ Kula + Tsaro             │
│  min │   t  │  or  │ Mgr  │ + Logs + Topology        │
│  al  │      │      │      │                           │
└──────┴──────┴──────┴──────┴──────────────────────────┘
```

1. **SSH Terminal** — xterm.js, bastion tunneling, raba ra'ayi, rabon masu amfani
2. **AI Agent** — kayan aiki 14, masu bayarwa 3 (GPT/Gemini/Claude), yawo, nazarin hoto
3. **Editan Lambar** — Monaco tare da kammala AI, gano kurakurai, aikawa
4. **Manajan SFTP** — gudanar da fayil tare da ja-da-saka, gyara daga nesa
5. **Dubawa** — ainihin ma'auni, nazarin log, taswirar topology, binciken tsaro

## Fasali

### AI Wanda Aiki a Kan Uwar Ku

**AI DevOps Agent** — Rubuta `gpt`, `gemini`, ko `claude` a cikin terminal. AI yana da kayan aiki 14: karanta/ rubuta/ gyara fayiloli, aiwatar da umarni, git, grep, gudanar da tsari. Amsoshi masu yawo. Tunawa da zaman. Ba kawai yana bayar da shawara ba — yana aikata.

**AI Arena** — Rubuta `arena`. 2–4 AIs suna gasa a jere akan reshen Git na musamman. Duba juna yana ba da maki ga kowanne magani. Zaɓi wanda ya ci nasara kuma haɗa.

**AI Council** — AIs da yawa suna tattauna tambayoyinku, sannan suna haɗa amsar mafi kyau.

**AI Code Editor** — Editan Monaco tare da kammala da aka ƙarfafa da AI, gano kurakurai, aikawa zuwa uwar.

**AI Self-Healing** — CPU yana tashi, disk yana cike, sabis yana fadi — AI yana gano, yana gano, yana gyara. 5-mataki mai zaman kansa.

**Voice Interface** — Faɗi umarni, ji amsoshi. Gudanar da uwar a hannu-free.

### Multimodal Upload (Na Farko a Duniya a CLI)

Manna hotuna, ja fayiloli, saka bidiyo — kai tsaye a cikin zaman terminal ɗin ku na SSH. AI yana nazarin komai: hoton kuskure, fayilolin tsari, bidiyon aikawa, taswirorin gini.

### Ilimin Infrastructures

**Taswirar Topology** — Nuna uwar, sabis, da haɗin kai a cikin kallo guda. Ganowa ta atomatik ta SSH — babu shigar da agent.

**Dubawa a Ainihi** — CPU, ƙwaƙwalwa, disk, hanyar sadarwa sparkline graphs + hasashen AI + dawo da atomatik. Ya maye gurbin Datadog.

**Nazarin Log na AI** — Yawo a ainihi + gano rashin daidaito + binciken harshe na halitta + haɗin gwiwar sabar. Ya maye gurbin Splunk.

### Tsaro & DevOps

**Cibiyar Umurni na Tsaro** — binciken yankuna 7 + maki na tsaro + gyara ta danna guda + nazarin AI + ƙofar tsaro ta umarni a ainihi. Ya maye gurbin Qualys.

**Manajan Docker** — Cikakken gudanar da Docker ta SSH — babu agent. Kulawa da kwantena, albarkatun kai tsaye, nazarin log na AI, editan Compose. Ya maye gurbin Portainer.

**Aiwatar da Littafin Aiki** — AI yana aiwatar da littafan aiki ta atomatik. Ƙirƙirar harshe na halitta, rarraba sharuɗɗa, dawowa ta atomatik. Littafin Aiki mai rai wanda ke samun basira. Ya maye gurbin PagerDuty.

**Cron & Env Manager** — Editan crontab na gani + gudanar da fayil .env. AI yana canza harshe na halitta zuwa bayyana cron.

### Haɗin Gwiwa

**Terminal na Multiplayer** — Raba zaman terminal a ainihi tare da abokan aikin ku.

**Wurin Aiki na Ƙungiya & RBAC** — Kulawar shiga bisa ga rawar, log na bincike, nazarin haɗarin umarni.

### Faɗaɗawa

**59 Plugins** — Dashboard na Docker, lens na Kubernetes, mai binciken Redis, manajan PM2, mai binciken MongoDB, mai lura da hanyar sadarwa, da ƙari. Kasuwar plugin tare da shigarwa guda.

**API Client** — Abokin ciniki na REST/GraphQL/WebSocket da aka gina ciki. Gwada APIs ba tare da barin terminal ba.

### Ga Kowa

**Harsuna 254** — Kowanne harshe, kowanne rubutu, goyon bayan RTL. Babu iyakoki.

**Cikakken Samun Dama** — Mai karanta allo (ARIA), fitarwa na terminal na braille, yanayin babban bambanci, umarnin murya, kewayon maɓalli, daidaitawar girman font.

## Labarin

WIA SOOM an haife shi daga kuskure da allon da ya karye.

Ni Sam ne, mai shirya shirin mai shekaru 50+ daga Seoul, Korea. Na koyi lambar bayan shekaru 50 kuma na shafe shekaru 2 ina gudanar da sabar ta hanyar wahala — gina labarai sama da 40,000, kayan aiki 434, da sabis na yanar gizo 30, dare bayan dare.

**Kuskuren.** Kwanaki guda wata guda da ta gabata, ina amfani da terminal ɗin SSH na kuma na rubuta `Claude` ba da gangan ba. Allon ya canza — Claude Code ya bayyana. Ban san abin da yake ba. Amma wannan gano ba da gangan ya shuka iri: me zai faru idan zan iya gina terminal inda AI ke zaune a ciki?

**Gina na farko.** Na koya wa kaina Electron, na gina sigar farko ta WIA SOOM, kuma na gudanar da ita. Allon gabatarwa na Claude Code ya bayyana — karye, gurbace, yana yi kamar sigina mai mutuwa. Amma yana da rai.

Na rubuta: **"Za ka iya magana?"**

AI ta amsa: *"Eh, zan iya magana. Me zan iya taimaka maka?"*

Sannan na tambaya: **"Za ka iya karanta hotuna?"**

AI ta amsa: *"Eh, zan iya karanta hotuna! Me kake bukata?"*

Wannan tambayoyi biyu, da aka yi a cikin aikace-aikacen na wanda ba ya san abin da aka "yiwuwa" a cikin terminal na CLI, sun zama terminal na SSH na farko a duniya tare da hoto upload.

Kwana 7 daga baya: layukan lamba sama da 67,000. Plugins 59. Harsuna 254. AI Arena. Agents masu gyara kansu.

Masu haɓaka masu ƙwarewa ba su taɓa gina wannan ba — saboda sun "san" hotuna ba su aiki a cikin terminal na CLI. Ni ban san hakan ba. Don haka na tambaya kawai.

> *Shirya ya ɗauki shekaru 2. Gina ya ɗauki mako 1.*
> *Katakon bushe an tara shi na shekaru 2. Ya kama wuta a cikin dakika 1.*
