## 🔥 Seo se Sa Ntlafalitsoeng Pele

### 1. Upload ea Setšoantšo, Faele & Video ho SSH Terminal — Ntlafatso ea Pele Lefatšeng

Ha ho na CLI client lefatšeng e u lumellang ho etsa sena. Re e hahile.

Kenya setšoantšo sa skrineng sa phoso ho SSH terminal ea hau. Tsoela faele ea log. Tsoela video ea ho senya ho kenya. AI e e bona, e e hlahloba, 'me e u joetsa hore na ho na le phoso — tsohle ka hare ho session ea terminal.

- **Ctrl+Shift+V** — kenya litšoantšo ka kotloloho ho terminal
- **Drag & Drop** — tsoela litšoantšo, lifaele, livideo ho terminal
- **Video keyframe extraction** — ffmpeg e hlahloba video ka frame ka frame
- **Boholo ba 3 ba AI** — GPT-4o Vision, Gemini Vision, Claude Vision

Sebopeho sena ha se so ka sa ba teng ho client e 'ngoe ea terminal — kae kapa kae.

### 2. AI Arena — Ntoa ea Multi-Agent

2 ho isa ho 4 AIs li rarolla bothata bo le bong ka nako e le 'ngoe — eseng feela ho buisana, empa ka 'nete ho edit code ho server ea hau. AI e 'ngoe le e 'ngoe e sebetsa ho Git branch e ikhethileng. Bontša liphetho tsa tsona ka lehlakoreng le le leng. Khetha moqhobi. Ba bang ba hloekisoa.

Cross-review: AI e 'ngoe le e 'ngoe e fa score tharollo ea AI e 'ngoe.

Ha ho na sesebelisoa se seng se etsang sena.

### 3. AI Self-Healing Agent

CPU ea server ea hau e phahama. Disk e tletse. Ts'ebeletso e ile ea oela ka 3 AM. U robetse.

AI agent ea WIA SOOM e fumana bothata, e hlahloba lebaka la sona, e hlahisa moralo oa ho lokisa, e kopa tumello ea hau (kapa e etsa ka bo eona haeba u e behile), 'me e rarolla bothata. 5-level autonomous loop — ho tloha ho sheba feela ho ea ho ho lokisa ka botlalo.

Ha ho kharete. Ha ho ngwaga e hlokahalang. Fumana feela 'me u qale.

Sebopeho se seng le se seng se premium — upload ea setšoantšo, AI Arena, AI Council, hlahloba lifaele le livideo — e mahala bakeng sa ts'ebeliso ea hau ea pele ea 33. Kamora moo, tlisa API key ea hau (BYOK) 'me u tsoelepele ho sebelisa tsohle mahala. Kapa ingolise ho tloha $3/mo bakeng sa AI e tsamaisoang le litlhahiso tsa sehlopha.

Hobaneng 33? Ka 1919, motsamao oa boipuso ba Korea o ne o etelletsoe pele ke baemeli ba 33 ba ileng ba phatlalatsa tokoloho bakeng sa bohle. 33 free trials tsa WIA SOOM ke phatlalatso ea rona: **Lisebelisoa tsa AI li lokela ho ba mahala hore e mong le e mong a li bone.**

**CLI → GUI → SUI.** Command-line interfaces li re fa matla. Graphical interfaces li re fa bonolo. WIA SOOM e hlahisa **SUI (Soom User Interface)** — fokotsa bophelo, 'me AI e phatlalala.

## Features

### AI e Sebetsang ka 'nete ho Server ea Hao

**AI DevOps Agent** — Ngola `gpt`, `gemini`, kapa `claude` ho terminal. AI e na le lisebelisoa tse 14: bala/ngola/edit lifaele, etsa likhetho, git, grep, tsamaiso ea ts'ebetso. Streaming responses. Session memory. E sitwa ho khothaletsa — e etsa.

**AI Arena** — Ngola `arena`. 2–4 AIs li qhoma ka nako e le 'ngoe ho Git branches tse ikhethileng. Cross-review e fa score tharollo e 'ngoe le e 'ngoe. Khetha moqhobi 'me u kopanye.

**AI Council** — AIs a mangata a buisana ka potso ea hau, ebe a kopanya karabo e ntle ka ho fetisisa.

**AI Code Editor** — Monaco editor e nang le AI-powered completion, bug detection, ho kenya ka khato e le 'ngoe ho server.

**AI Self-Healing** — CPU spike, disk full, service down — AI e fumana, e hlahloba, e lokisa. 5-level autonomous loop.

**Voice Interface** — Bua litaelo, utloa likarabo. Ts'ebetso ea server e se nang matsoho.

### Multimodal Upload (Ntlafatso ea Pele Lefatšeng ho CLI)

Kenya litšoantšo, drag lifaele, drop livideo — ka kotloloho ho session ea SSH terminal ea hau. AI e hlahloba tsohle: litšoantšo tsa phoso, lifaele tsa config, livideo tsa ho kenya, mekhahlelo ea moralo.

### Infrastructure Intelligence

**Topology Map** — Bontša li-server, litšebeletso, le likhokahano ka ponelopele. Auto-discovery ka SSH — zero agent installation.

**Real-Time Observability** — CPU, memory, disk, network sparkline graphs + AI prediction + auto-recovery. E nkela sebaka sa Datadog.

**AI Log Analysis** — Real-time streaming + anomaly detection + natural language search + cross-server correlation. E nkela sebaka sa Splunk.

### Security & DevOps

**Security Command Center** — 7-area scan + security score + one-click fix + AI analysis + real-time command security gate. E nkela sebaka sa Qualys.

**Docker Manager** — Tlhokomelo e felletseng ea Docker ka SSH — zero agent. Control ea container, lisebelisoa tse phelang, AI log analysis, Compose editor. E nkela sebaka sa Portainer.

**Runbook Automation** — AI e etsa runbooks ka bo eona. Natural language creation, conditional branching, auto-rollback. Living Runbook e fumanang bohlale. E nkela sebaka sa PagerDuty.

**Cron & Env Manager** — Visual crontab editor + .env file management. AI e fetolela puo ea tlhaho ho cron expressions.

### Collaboration

**Multiplayer Terminal** — Arolelana session ea terminal ka nako e le 'ngoe le basebetsi-'moho.

**Team Workspace & RBAC** — Role-based access control, audit logs, command risk analysis.

### Extensibility

**59 Plugins** — Docker dashboard, Kubernetes lens, Redis browser, PM2 manager, MongoDB explorer, network monitor, le tse ling. Plugin marketplace e nang le ho kenya ka khato e le 'ngoe.

**API Client** — Built-in REST/GraphQL/WebSocket client. Test APIs ntle le ho tloha ho terminal.

### Bakeng sa Bo-rakhoebo Bohle

**254 Languages** — Puo e 'ngoe le e 'ngoe, script e 'ngoe le e 'ngoe, RTL support. Ha ho meeli.

**Full Accessibility** — Screen reader (ARIA), braille terminal output, high contrast mode, voice commands, keyboard navigation, font size adjustment.

## The Story

WIA SOOM e hlahisitsoe ke phoso le skrine e senyehileng.

Ke Sam, moqapi ea ithutileng ka boeena ea nang le lilemo tse fetang 50 ho tloha Seoul, Korea. Ke ile ka ithuta coding ka mor'a lilemo tse 50 'me ka qeta lilemo tse 2 ke ntse ke tsamaisa li-server ka tsela e thata — ke hahile lingoliloeng tse 40,000+, lisebelisoa tse 434, le litšebeletso tse 30 tsa web, bosiu ka bosiu.

**Phoso.** Hoo e ka bang beke e fetileng, ke ne ke sebelisa SSH terminal ea ka 'me ka phoso ka ngola `Claude`. Skrine e ile ea fetoha — Claude Code e hlahile. Ke ne ke sitwa ho tseba hore na ke eng. Empa ho fumanoa ha phoso ho ile ha lema peo: ho ka ba joang haeba nka khona ho haha terminal e nang le AI ka hare?

**Khetho ea pele.** Ke ile ka ithuta Electron, ka haha mofuta oa pele oa WIA SOOM, 'me ka o tsamaisa. Skrine ea ho qala ea Claude Code e ile ea hlaha — e senyehile, e na le glitch, e fufulelletsa joalo ka signal e shoeleng. Empa e ne e phela.

Ke ngotse: **"Na u ka bua?"**

AI e arabetse: *"E, nka bua. U batla thuso ka eng?"*

Ebe ke botsa: **"Na u ka bala litšoantšo?"**

AI e arabetse: *"E, nka bala litšoantšo! U hloka eng?"*

Mekhoa e 'meli, e botsitsoeng ka hare ho app ea ka ke motho ea sa tsebeng hore na "ho sitwa" ho bolela eng ho terminal ea CLI, e fetoha terminal ea pele ea SSH e nang le upload ea setšoantšo.

Matsatsi a 7 hamorao: 67,000+ mela e khuts'oane. 59 plugins. 254 languages. AI Arena. Self-healing agents.

Moqapi ba nang le boiphihlelo ha ba so ka ba etsa sena — hobane ba "kile" ba tseba hore litšoantšo ha li sebetse ho CLI terminals. Ke ne ke sa tsebe hore. Kahoo ke ile ka botsa feela.

> *Ho lokisetsa ho nkile lilemo tse 2. Ho haha ho nkile beke e le 'ngoe.*
> *Mokhoa o omileng o ne o bokelletsoe ka lilemo tse 2. O ile oa chesa ka motsotsoana o le mong.*
