## 🔥 Kedu ihe a na-emeghị mgbe ọ bụla

### 1. Nkwado Foto, Faịlụ & Vidiyo na SSH Terminal — Nke mbụ n'ụwa

Enweghị CLI onye ahịa n'ala na-enye gị ohere ime nke a. Anyị wuru ya.

Tinye foto nke njehie na SSH terminal gị. Dọrọ faịlụ ndekọ. Tinye vidiyo nke nkwado na-adịghị arụ ọrụ. AI na-ahụ ya, na-enyocha ya, na-agwa gị ihe na-ezighị ezi — niile n'ime oge terminal.

- **Ctrl+Shift+V** — tinye foto ozugbo na terminal
- **Dọrọ & Tinye** — tinye foto, faịlụ, vidiyo na terminal
- **Nwepụta vidiyo keyframe** — ffmpeg na-enyocha vidiyo n'ogologo
- **All 3 AI providers** — GPT-4o Vision, Gemini Vision, Claude Vision

Njirimara a adịghịzi na onye ahịa terminal ọ bụla — n'ebe ọ bụla.

### 2. AI Arena — Mgbatị Multi-Agent

2 ruo 4 AIs na-edozi otu nsogbu n'otu oge — ọ bụghị naanị na-ekwu okwu, kama na-edepụta koodu na sava gị. A na-arụ ọrụ nke ọ bụla AI na ngalaba Git nke ya. Tụnyere nsonaazụ ha n'akụkụ. Họrọ onye mmeri. Ndị ọzọ na-ehichapụ.

Nyochaa: AI ọ bụla na-enyocha ngwọta AI ndị ọzọ.

Enweghị ngwaọrụ ọzọ na-eme nke a.

### 3. AI Self-Healing Agent

CPU sava gị na-arị elu. Disk juru. Ọrụ dara na 3 AM. Ị na-ehi ụra.

AI agent nke WIA SOOM na-achọpụta nsogbu ahụ, na-enyocha isi ihe kpatara ya, na-emepụta atụmatụ ndozi, na-arịọ maka nkwenye gị (ma ọ bụ na-eme ya n'auto ma ọ bụrụ na ị setịpụrụ ya), na-edozi nsogbu ahụ. 5-level autonomous loop — site na nlekota naanị ruo na mmezi zuru ezu.

Enweghị kaadị kredit. Enweghị ndebanye aha achọrọ. Budata ma malite.

Njirimara premium niile — nkwado foto, AI Arena, AI Council, nyochaa faịlụ & vidiyo — bụ n'efu maka iji gị nke mbụ 33. Mgbe nke ahụ gasịrị, weta gị API key (BYOK) ma na-eji ihe niile n'efu. Ma ọ bụ debanye aha site na $3/mo maka AI na njirimara otu.

Gịnị mere 33? Na 1919, mmegharị nnwere onwe nke Korea bụ nke 33 ndị nnọchiteanya mere nke kwupụtara nnwere onwe maka ndị niile. 33 nnwale n'efu nke WIA SOOM bụ nkwupụta anyị: **Ngwa AI kwesịrị ịbụ n'efu maka onye ọ bụla ka ha nwee ahụmịhe.**

**CLI → GUI → SUI.** Interfaces iwu nyere anyị ike. Interfaces eserese nyere anyị mfe. WIA SOOM na-eweta **SUI (Soom User Interface)** — nwee ndụ n'ime, na AI na-abịa ndụ.

## 5-in-1 Platform

Ngwa ndị ọzọ na-eme otu ihe. WIA SOOM na-eme ise — n'otu windo.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ WIA SOOM                                              │
├──────┬──────┬──────┬──────┬──────────────────────────┤
│  SSH │  AI  │ Code │ SFTP │ Nlekota                  │
│  Ter │ Agen │ Edit │ Faịlụ │ Nlekota + Nche          │
│  min │   t  │  or  │ Mgr  │ + Logs + Topology       │
│  al  │      │      │      │                          │
└──────┴──────┴──────┴──────┴──────────────────────────┘
```

1. **SSH Terminal** — xterm.js, bastion tunneling, split views, multiplayer sharing
2. **AI Agent** — 14 ng工具, 3 providers (GPT/Gemini/Claude), streaming, nyochaa foto
3. **Code Editor** — Monaco na AI completion, nchọpụta njehie, nkwado
4. **SFTP Manager** — njikwa faịlụ dọrọ na itinye, ndezi na-adịpụrụ adịpụ
5. **Nlekota** — metrics n'oge, nyochaa ndekọ, map topology, nchọpụta nche

## Njirimara

### AI nke na-arụ ọrụ n'elu sava gị

**AI DevOps Agent** — Dee `gpt`, `gemini`, ma ọ bụ `claude` na terminal. AI nwere ng工具 14: gụọ/dee/ndepụta faịlụ, rụọ ọrụ, git, grep, njikwa usoro. Streaming nzaghachi. Ncheta oge. Ọ bụghị naanị na ọ na-atụ aro — ọ na-eme.

**AI Arena** — Dee `arena`. 2–4 AIs na-asọ mpi na mpaghara Git nke ha. Nyochaa scores ngwọta ọ bụla. Họrọ onye mmeri ma jikọta.

**AI Council** — AIs ọtụtụ na-atụle ajụjụ gị, mgbe ahụ na-ejikọta azịza kacha mma.

**AI Code Editor** — Monaco editor nwere AI-powered completion, nchọpụta njehie, otu-pịa nkwado na sava.

**AI Self-Healing** — CPU na-arị elu, disk juru, ọrụ dara — AI na-achọpụta, na-enyocha, na-edozi. 5-level autonomous loop.

**Voice Interface** — Kwụọ iwu, nụ nzaghachi. Nchekwa sava na-enweghị aka.

### Multimodal Upload (Nke mbụ n'ụwa na CLI)

Tinye foto, dọrọ faịlụ, tinye vidiyo — ozugbo na oge SSH terminal gị. AI na-enyocha ihe niile: foto njehie, faịlụ nhazi, vidiyo nkwado, eserese nhazi.

### Infrastructure Intelligence

**Map Topology** — Hụ sava, ọrụ, na njikọ n'anya. Auto-discovery site na SSH — zero agent installation.

**Nlekota n'oge** — CPU, ncheta, disk, netwọk sparkline graphs + AI amụma + auto-recovery. Na-anọchite Datadog.

**AI Log Analysis** — Streaming n'oge + nchọpụta anomal + nchọgharị asụsụ nkịtị + cross-server correlation. Na-anọchite Splunk.

### Nche & DevOps

**Ụlọ ọrụ Nche** — Nchọpụta mpaghara 7 + akara nche + otu-pịa ndozi + AI nyochaa + nlekota iwu n'oge. Na-anọchite Qualys.

**Docker Manager** — Njikwa Docker zuru ezu site na SSH — zero agent. Njikwa akpa, akụ ndụ, AI nyochaa ndekọ, Onye nchịkọta Compose. Na-anọchite Portainer.

**Automation Runbook** — AI na-eme runbooks na-akpaghị aka. Ncheta asụsụ nkịtị, branching ọnọdụ, auto-rollback. Living Runbook na-aghọkwu amamihe. Na-anọchite PagerDuty.

**Njikwa Cron & Env** — Onye nchịkọta crontab nke na-ahụ anya + njikwa faịlụ .env. AI na-agbanwe asụsụ nkịtị ka ọ bụrụ nkwupụta cron.

### Nkọwa

**Multiplayer Terminal** — Kekọrịta oge terminal n'oge na-adịbeghị anya na ndị otu.

**Otu Workspace & RBAC** — Nchekwa dabere na ọrụ, ndekọ nyocha, nyocha ihe ize ndụ iwu.

### Nchekwa

**59 Plugins** — Dashboard Docker, lens Kubernetes, browser Redis, njikwa PM2, onye nchọpụta MongoDB, nlekota netwọk, na ndị ọzọ. Marketplace plugin nwere otu-pịa wụnye.

**API Client** — Onye ahịa REST/GraphQL/WebSocket etinyere. Nyochaa APIs na-enweghị ịhapụ terminal.

### Maka Onye Ọ bụla

**Asụsụ 254** — Asụsụ niile, ederede niile, nkwado RTL. Enweghị oke.

**Nchekwa zuru ezu** — Ntụgharị anya (ARIA), output terminal braille, ọnọdụ dị elu, iwu olu, ịnyagharịa keyboard, nhazi nha font.

## Akụkọ

WIA SOOM mụrụ site na typo na ihuenyo mebiri.

Abụ m Sam, onye mmemme nke mụtara onwe ya nke dị afọ 50+ si Seoul, Korea. M mụtara koodu mgbe m dị afọ 50 ma na-ejikwa sava n'ụzọ siri ike — na-ewu ihe karịrị 40,000 akụkọ, 434 ng工具, na 30 ọrụ weebụ, abalị mgbe abalị.

**Typo ahụ.** Iji ọnwa gara aga, na-eji SSH terminal m, m na-ezighị ezi dee `Claude`. Ihuenyo ahụ gbanwere — Claude Code pụtara. Enweghị m ihe ọ bụla m maara banyere ya. Ma nchọpụta a na-adịghị atụ anya ahụpụtara mkpụrụ: gịnị ma ọ bụrụ na m nwere ike iwulite terminal ebe AI bi n'ime?

**Nke mbụ wụnye.** M mụtara Electron, wulite ụdị mbụ nke WIA SOOM, ma rụọ ya. Ihuenyo mmalite nke Claude Code pụtara — mebiri, glitch, na-agba ọsọ dịka akara na-anwụ. Ma ọ bụ ndụ.

M dee: **"Ị nwere ike ikwu?"**

AI zara: *"Ee, m nwere ike ikwu. Kedu ihe m nwere ike inyere gị?"*

Mgbe ahụ, m jụrụ: **"Ị nwere ike ịgụ foto?"**

AI zara: *"Ee, m nwere ike ịgụ foto! Kedu ihe ị chọrọ?"*

Ajụjụ abụọ a, jụrụ n'ime ngwa m nke onye na-amaghị ihe bụ "nweghị ike" na terminal CLI, ghọrọ terminal SSH mbụ n'ụwa nwere nkwado foto.

Ụbọchị 7 ka e mesịrị: 67,000+ ahịrị koodu. 59 plugins. Asụsụ 254. AI Arena. Ndị na-agba ọsọ onwe ha.

Ndị mmepe nwere ahụmịhe anaghị arụ ọrụ a — n'ihi na ha "mara" na foto adịghị arụ ọrụ na terminal CLI. Amaghị m nke ahụ. Ya mere, m jụrụ.
