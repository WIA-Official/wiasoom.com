## 🔥 I yini Leswi Ku Hlumeliweke

### 1. Ku Hlawula Swifaniso, Mafayela & Mavhidhiyo e SSH Terminal — Ku Hlumeliweke Kwalaha

A ku na CLI client e misava leyi ku nga ku letela ku endla leswi. Hi ku humelela.

Hlawula xifaniso xa ku humelela e terminal ya wena ya SSH. Hlawula faayela ya log. Dlayisa video ya ku humelela. AI yi yi vona, yi yi hlaya, naswona yi ku byela leswi nga riki kahle — hinkwaswo hi ku ringana na terminal session.

- **Ctrl+Shift+V** — hlawula swifaniso hi ku hatlisa e terminal
- **Hlawula & Dlayisa** — dlayisa swifaniso, mafayela, mavhidhiyo e terminal
- **Ku Hlawula Mavhidhiyo** — ffmpeg yi hlaya video frame hi frame
- **Tihlanganisi ta 3 ta AI** — GPT-4o Vision, Gemini Vision, Claude Vision

Minkombiso leyi a yi ri kona hi CLI client — kwalaho.

### 2. AI Arena — Ku Hlangana ka Ma-Agent

2 ku ya 4 AIs a va endla ntirho wun'wana hi nkarhi wun'wana — a va nga vulavula ntsena, kambe va endla ku hlela code e server ya wena. AI yin'wana yi tirha e Git branch leyi yaka. Hlanganisa swiphemu swa vona hi ku ringana. Hlawula loyi a winaka. Loyi a nga na swona a yi ta humelela.

Ku hlaya: AI yin'wana yi hlaya mpfuno wa AI yin'wana.

A ku na xitlhangu xin'wana lexi endlaka leswi.

### 3. AI Self-Healing Agent

CPU ya server ya wena yi phindzelela. Disk yi helela. Ntlhanu yi hlengeletiwa hi 3 AM. U le ku robala.

AI agent ya WIA SOOM yi vona xiphiqo, yi hlaya xiphiqo, yi endla nhlahla, yi kombela ku pfumala kwen'we (leswi u swi veke), naswona yi xava xiphiqo. 5-level autonomous loop — ku suka eka ku langutela ntsena ku ya eka ku pfuxeta.

## Minkombiso

### AI Leyi Tiyela Ku Humelela e Server Ya Wena

**AI DevOps Agent** — Tipe `gpt`, `gemini`, kumbe `claude` e terminal. AI yi na 14 tools: ku hlaya/ku tsala/ku hlela mafayela, ku endla mirhi, git, grep, ku langutela. Streaming responses. Session memory. A yi nga tsundzuki ntsena — yi endla.

**AI Arena** — Tipe `arena`. 2–4 AIs a va hlangana hi nkarhi wun'wana e Git branches. Ku hlaya ku hlangana ku hlaya mpfuno wa switlhangu. Hlawula loyi a winaka na ku hlanganisa.

**AI Council** — AIs yo tala a va vulavula hi xihlawuhlawu xa wena, a va hlanganisa mpfuno wa kahle.

**AI Code Editor** — Monaco editor na AI-powered completion, bug detection, ku humelela hi ku hatlisa e server.

**AI Self-Healing** — CPU spike, disk full, service down — AI yi vona, yi hlaya, yi humelela. 5-level autonomous loop.

**Voice Interface** — Tiva mirhi, twa mpfuno. Ku langutela server hi ku nga ri na matlhari.

### Multimodal Upload (Ku Hlumeliweke Kwalaha e CLI)

Hlawula swifaniso, drag mafayela, dlayisa mavhidhiyo — hi ku hatlisa e terminal ya wena ya SSH. AI yi hlaya hinkwaswo: swifaniso swa xiphiqo, mafayela ya config, mavhidhiyo ya ku humelela, swifaniso swa vuhlayiseki.

### Infrastructure Intelligence

**Topology Map** — Hlanganyela switlhangu, switlhangu, na ku hlanganisa hi ku langutela. Ku hlanganisa hi SSH — zero agent installation.

**Ku Langutela Nkarhi Na Nkarhi** — CPU, memory, disk, network sparkline graphs + ku humelela ka AI + ku humelela ka xitlhangu. Ku humelela Datadog.

**AI Log Analysis** — Ku langutela nkarhi na nkarhi + ku humelela ka xiphiqo + ku hlaya ka ririmi ra ntlhanu + ku hlanganisa ka switlhangu. Ku humelela Splunk.

### Xitlhangu & DevOps

**Xitlhangu xa Ku Command** — ku hlaya ka 7 + xitlhangu xa xitlhangu + ku humelela hi ku hatlisa + AI hlaya + ku langutela nkarhi na nkarhi. Ku humelela Qualys.

**Docker Manager** — Ku hlanganisa Docker hi SSH — zero agent. Ku langutela ka container, switlhangu leswi hlanganisiweke, ku hlaya ka log ya AI, Compose editor. Ku humelela Portainer.

**Runbook Automation** — AI yi endla runbooks hi ku hatlisa. Ku hlanganisa ka ririmi ra ntlhanu, ku hlanganisa ka xitlhangu, ku humelela hi ku hatlisa. Runbook leyi endlaka ku va ni vutlhari. Ku humelela PagerDuty.

**Cron & Env Manager** — Visual crontab editor + .env file management. AI yi hlanganisa ririmi ra ntlhanu na ku hlanganisa ka cron.

### Ku Hlanganyela

**Multiplayer Terminal** — Hlanganisa terminal sessions hi nkarhi na nkarhi na vatirhi va wena.

**Team Workspace & RBAC** — Ku hlanganisa ka vuhlayiseki, audit logs, ku hlaya ka xitlhangu.

### Ku Hlanganisa

**59 Plugins** — Docker dashboard, Kubernetes lens, Redis browser, PM2 manager, MongoDB explorer, network monitor, na switlhangu swo karhi. Plugin marketplace na ku hlanganisa hi ku hatlisa.

**API Client** — API client yo hlanganisi (REST/GraphQL/WebSocket). Test APIs u nga si hamba e terminal.

### Hi Vona Vunene

**254 Tihlanganisi** — Ririmi hinkwaro, xitlhangu hinkwaro, RTL support. A ku na mihlovo.

**Ku Hlanganisa Kwalaha** — Screen reader (ARIA), braille terminal output, high contrast mode, voice commands, keyboard navigation, font size adjustment.

## Xifaniso

WIA SOOM yi velekiwe hi typo na xitshuketa lexi hundzuke.

Ndzawulo ya mina i Sam, programmer ya 50+ leyi dyondzeke hi ku tirha e Seoul, Korea. Ndzi dyondze ku tsala hi nkarhi wa 50 naswona ndzi tirhisa 2 malembe ku hlanganisa switlhangu hi ndlela yo tika — ku humelela 40,000+ swifaniso, 434 tools, na 30 web services, nhloko hi nhloko.

**Typo.** Hi vhiki ra xihlawuhlawu, ndzi ri karhi ndzi tirhisa terminal ya mina ya SSH naswona ndzi hatlisa tsala `Claude`. Xitshuketa xi hundzuke — Claude Code yi humile. A ndzi ri na mhaka ya leswi. Kambe ku humelela kwale ku humelela ku veka xitsundzuxo: ku yini loko ndzi nga humelela terminal leyi AI yi tshama eka yona?

**Ku humelela ku sungula.** Ndzi dyondze Electron, ndzi humelela verson ya ku sungula ya WIA SOOM, naswona ndzi yi endla. Xitshuketa xa Claude Code xi humile — ku hundzuka, ku humelela, ku phindzelela tanihi xitsundzuxo lexi feke. Kambe yi ve ni vutomi.

Ndzi tsale: **"U nga vulavula?"**

AI yi hlamule: *"E-e, ndzi nga vulavula. U nga ndzi pfuna na yini?"*

Endzhaku ka sweswo ndzi vutisa: **"U nga hlaya swifaniso?"**

AI yi hlamule: *"E-e, ndzi nga hlaya swifaniso! U lava yini?"*

Minkombiso mimbirhi leyi, leyi ndzi yi vutiseke e app ya mina hi munhu loyi a nga tiva leswi "nga riki kahle" e terminal ya CLI, yi ve terminal ya SSH ya ku humelela swifaniso.

Mavhiki ya 7: 67,000+ mintlhave. 59 plugins. 254 tihlanganisi. AI Arena. Ma-agent ya self-healing.

Vaendli va switlhangu va nga ri na leswi — hikuva va "vona" swifaniso a swi tirhi e terminal ya CLI. A ndzi nga ri na mhaka ya sweswo. Hikwalaho, ndzi vutisile.
