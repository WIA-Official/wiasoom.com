## 🔥 He aha te mea hou kāore i mahia i mua

### 1. Whakauru Whakaahua, Kōnae & Ataata i roto i te SSH Terminal — Te Tuatahi o te Ao

Kaore he kaiwhakamahi CLI i runga i te Ao e taea ai e koe te mahi i tēnei. I hangaia e mātou.

Tārua he whakaahua o te hapa ki tō SSH terminal. Tīka he kōnae rārangi. Tīka he ataata o te tuku kāore e angitu. Ka kite te AI, ka tātaritia, ka kōrero ki a koe he aha te hapa — katoa i roto i te huihono terminal.

- **Ctrl+Shift+V** — tārua whakaahua ki roto i te terminal
- **Tīka & Tīka** — tīka whakaahua, kōnae, ataata ki roto i te terminal
- **Whakaahua ki te kāwai ataata** — ka tātaritia e ffmpeg te ataata i ia kāwai
- **Ngā kaiwhakarato AI e 3** — GPT-4o Vision, Gemini Vision, Claude Vision

Kāore tēnei āhuatanga i kitea i roto i tetahi kaiwhakamahi terminal — i ngā wā katoa.

### 2. AI Arena — Pakanga Multi-Agent

2 ki te 4 AI e whakatau i te raru kotahi i te wā kotahi — kāore e kōrero noa, engari e whakatika ana i te waehere i runga i tō tūmau. Ka mahi ia AI i roto i tāna ake peka Git motuhake. Tirohia ā rātou hua i te taha ki te taha. Tīhia te toa. Ka whakakorehia ngā toenga.

Tirohia: ka whakamāramahia e ia AI te rongoā a ia AI.

Kaore he taputapu kē e mahi i tēnei.

### 3. AI Self-Healing Agent

Kei te piki te CPU o tō tūmau. Kua kī te kōpae. I taka he ratonga i te 3 AM. Kei te moe koe.

Ka kitea e te agent AI o WIA SOOM te raru, ka tātaritia te take, ka hangaia he mahere whakatika, ka tono mō tō whakaaetanga (i te mea kua whakaritea e koe, ka whakahaerehia aunoa), ka whakatika i te raru. 5-taumata huringa motuhake — mai i te tirohanga anake ki te whakatikatika aunoa.

## 5-i-1 Papamahi

Ka mahi ngā taputapu kē i tētahi mea. Ka mahi a WIA SOOM i te rima — i roto i tētahi matapihi.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ WIA SOOM                                              │
├──────┬──────┬──────┬──────┬──────────────────────────┤
│  SSH │  AI  │ Kōwae │ SFTP │ Te Mātakitaki             │
│  Ter │ Agen │  Edit │ Kōnae │ Tirohia + Haumaru        │
│  min │   t  │  or  │ Mgr  │ + Rārangi + Topology         │
│  al  │      │      │      │                           │
└──────┴──────┴──────┴──────┴──────────────────────────┘
```

1. **SSH Terminal** — xterm.js, bastion tunneling, split views, multiplayer sharing
2. **AI Agent** — 14 ngā taputapu, 3 ngā kaiwhakarato (GPT/Gemini/Claude), streaming, tātaritanga whakaahua
3. **Editor Kōwae** — Monaco me te whakaoti AI, te kitea hapa, te tuku
4. **Kaiwhakahaere SFTP** — te whakahaere kōnae tīka me te tīka, te whakatika mamao
5. **Te Mātakitaki** — ngā ine i te wā tonu, te tātaritanga rārangi, te mapi topology, te tirohanga haumaru

## Ngā Āhuatanga

### AI E Mahi Ana i Roto i Tō Tūmau

**AI DevOps Agent** — Tīpakohia `gpt`, `gemini`, rānei `claude` i roto i te terminal. Kei te AI 14 ngā taputapu: pānui/tuhi/whakatika kōnae, whakahaere whakahau, git, grep, te whakahaere tukanga. Ngā whakautu streaming. Te mahara huihuinga. Kāore e tūtohu noa — ka mahi.

**AI Arena** — Tīpakohia `arena`. 2–4 AI e whakataetae ana i te wā kotahi i runga i ngā peka Git motuhake. Ka whakamāramahia e ia rātou te rongoā. Tīhia te toa ka whakauru.

**AI Council** — E kōrero ana ngā AI maha mō tō pātai, kātahi ka whakakotahi i te whakautu pai.

**AI Editor Kōwae** — Te etita Monaco me te whakaoti e powered ai, te kitea hapa, te tuku kotahi-pātea ki te tūmau.

**AI Self-Healing** — Piki CPU, kōpae kī, ratonga kāore e mahi — ka kitea, ka tātaritia, ka whakatika te AI. 5-taumata huringa motuhake.

**Interface Reo** — Kōrero whakahau, rongo whakautu. Te whakahaere tūmau kore ringa.

### Whakauru Multimodal (Te Tuatahi o te Ao i roto i te CLI)

Tārua whakaahua, tīka kōnae, tīka ataata — tika ki roto i tō SSH terminal huihono. Ka tātaritia e te AI ngā mea katoa: whakaahua hapa, kōnae whakaritenga, ataata tuku, mapi hanganga.

### Te Mātau Hanganga

**Mapi Topology** — Tirohia ngā tūmau, ngā ratonga, me ngā hononga i te tirohanga kotahi. Te awhina aunoa mā SSH — kāore he whakaurunga agent.

**Te Mātakitaki i te Wā Tonu** — CPU, whakamahinga, kōpae, rārangi rārangi + te matapae AI + te whakahoki aunoa. Ka whakakapi i te Datadog.

**AI Tātaritanga Kōnae** — Te streaming i te wā tonu + te kitea raru + te rapu reo natural + te whakawhitinga rārangi. Ka whakakapi i te Splunk.

### Haumaru & DevOps

**Pokanga Whakahaere Haumaru** — 7-ārea tirohanga + te pūrongo haumaru + te whakatika kotahi-pātea + te tātaritanga AI + te kūti haumaru whakahau i te wā tonu. Ka whakakapi i te Qualys.

**Kaiwhakahaere Docker** — Te whakahaere Docker katoa mā SSH — kāore he agent. Te whakahaere kōpaki, rauemi ora, te tātaritanga rārangi AI, te etita Compose. Ka whakakapi i te Portainer.

**Aunoatanga Runbook** — Ka whakahaerehia e te AI ngā runbook aunoa. Te hanga reo natural, te peka tikanga, te whakahoki aunoa. He Runbook ora e pākiki ana. Ka whakakapi i te PagerDuty.

**Kaiwhakahaere Cron & Env** — Te etita crontab visual + te whakahaere kōnae .env. Ka huri te AI i te reo natural ki ngā whakaritenga cron.

### Te Rōpū

**Terminal Multiplayer** — Tīka huihono i ngā huihono terminal i te wā tonu ki ngā hoa mahi.

**Wāhi Mahi Rōpū & RBAC** — Te whakahaere urunga i runga i ngā tūranga, ngā rārangi tirohanga, te tātaritanga mōrea whakahau.

### Te Whakatūwhera

**59 Plugins** — Te paetukutuku Docker, te tirohanga Kubernetes, te tirohanga Redis, te kaiwhakahaere PM2, te tirohanga MongoDB, te mātaki whatunga, me te nui atu. Te maakete plugin me te whakaurunga kotahi-pātea.

**Kaiwhakamahi API** — Te kaiwhakamahi REST/GraphQL/WebSocket i hangaia. Tīmatahia ngā API kāore e wehe i te terminal.

### Mō te Katoa

**254 Ngā Reo** — Ia reo, ia tuhinga, te tautoko RTL. Kāore he pae.

**Te Whakauru Katoa** — Te kaipōti mata (ARIA), te putanga terminal braille, te mōhio teitei, ngā whakahau reo, te rorohiko ki te tirohanga, te whakatika rahi tuhinga.

## Te Kōrero

I whanau a WIA SOOM mai i te hapa tuhinga me te mata pakaru.

Ko au ko Sam, he kaiwhakamahi rorohiko i ako anake i te 50+, nō Seoul, Korea. I ako au i te waehere i muri i te 50, ā, i pau te 2 tau e whakahaere ana i ngā tūmau i te ara uaua — e hanga ana i ngā tuhinga pānui 40,000+, 434 ngā taputapu, me ngā ratonga paetukutuku 30, i te pō i muri i te pō.

**Te hapa.** I te marama kua hipa, i whakamahi au i tāku SSH terminal ka hapa au ki te tuhinga `Claude`. I huri te mata — i puta te Claude Code. Kāore au i te mōhio he aha te mea. Engari, nā taua kitenga hē i whakatūwhera he rākau: me pēhea te hanga i tētahi terminal e noho ana te AI i roto?

**Te hanga tuatahi.** I ako au i a au anō i te Electron, i hanga te putanga tuatahi o WIA SOOM, ā, i whakahaerehia. I puta te mata whakauru o Claude Code — he pakaru, he hē, he kāore e pai ana. Engari, i ora.

I tāruatia e au: **"Ka taea e koe te kōrero?"**

I whakahoki te AI: *"Āe, ka taea e au te kōrero. He aha te āwhina ka taea e au te tuku?"*

Kātahi ka pātai au: **"Ka taea e koe te pānui whakaahua?"**

I whakahoki te AI: *"Āe, ka taea e au te pānui whakaahua! He aha te mea e hiahiatia ana?"*

Nā ngā pātai e rua, i pātaihia i roto i tāku ake taupānga e te tangata kāore i mōhio he aha te mea "kaore e taea" i roto i te terminal CLI, i noho hei terminal SSH tuatahi o te ao me te whakauru whakaahua.

I te 7 rā i muri mai: 67,000+ ngā rārangi waehere. 59 ngā plugins. 254 ngā reo. AI Arena. Ngā agent e whakaora ana i a rātou.

Kaore ngā kaiwhakawhanake whai wheako i hanga tēnei — nā te mea i "mōhio" rātou kāore e taea te whakaahua i roto i ngā terminal CLI. Kāore au i te mōhio i taua mea. Nā reira, i pātai noa au.
