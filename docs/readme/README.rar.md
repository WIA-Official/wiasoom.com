## 🔥 He aha te mea hou kāore i mahia i mua

### 1. Whakauru Whakaahua, Kōnae & Ataata i roto i te SSH Terminal — Te Tuatahi i te Ao

Kāore he kaiwhakamahi CLI i runga i te Ao e taea ai e koe tēnei. I hangaia e mātou.

Whakauru he whakaahua o te hapa ki tō SSH terminal. Tākina he kōnae rārangi. Tākina he ataata o te tuku kāore e angitu. Ka kite te AI, ka tātaritia, ka kī atu ki a koe he aha te hapa — katoa i roto i te wāhanga terminal.

- **Ctrl+Shift+V** — whakauru whakaahua tika ki roto i te terminal
- **Tākina & Tākina** — tākina ngā whakaahua, ngā kōnae, ngā ataata ki roto i te terminal
- **Whakaahua keyframe tango** — ka tātaritia e ffmpeg te ataata i te taumata
- **Ngā kaiwhakarato AI e 3** — GPT-4o Vision, Gemini Vision, Claude Vision

Kāore tēnei āhuatanga i kitea i roto i tetahi kaiwhakamahi terminal — i ngā wā katoa.

### 2. AI Arena — Pakanga Multi-Agent

2 ki te 4 AI e whakaoti ana i te raru kotahi i te wā kotahi — kāore e kōrero noa, engari e whakatika ana i te waehere i runga i tō rorohiko. Ka mahi ia AI i roto i tāna ake pākete Git motuhake. Tirohia ngā hua e rua i te taha. Tīhia te toa. Ka whakakorehia ngā toenga.

Tirohanga pātea: ka tāpaea e ia AI te tohu i ngā rongoā a ia atu AI.

Kāore he taputapu kē e mahi i tēnei.

### 3. AI Self-Healing Agent

Kei te piki te CPU o tō rorohiko. Kua kī te kōpae. I pakaru he ratonga i te 3 AM. Kei te moe koe.

Ka kitea e te agent AI o WIA SOOM te raru, ka tātaritia te take, ka hangaia he mahere whakatika, ka tono mō tō whakaaetanga (rānei ka whakahaerehia aunoa mēnā kua whakaritea e koe), ka whakatika i te raru. 5-level autonomous loop — mai i te tirohanga anake ki te whakatikatika aunoa.

## 5-i-1 Pātaka

Ka mahi ngā taputapu kē i tētahi mea. Ka mahi a WIA SOOM i te rima — i roto i tētahi matapihi.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ WIA SOOM                                              │
├──────┬──────┬──────┬──────┬──────────────────────────┤
│  SSH │  AI  │ Tuhinga │ SFTP │ Te Mātai               │
│  Ter │ Agen │  Kōwae  │ Kōnae │ Tirohia + Haumaru      │
│  min │   t  │        │      │ + Rārangi + Topology    │
│  al  │      │        │      │                           │
└──────┴──────┴──────┴──────┴──────────────────────────┘
```

1. **SSH Terminal** — xterm.js, bastion tunneling, split views, multiplayer sharing
2. **AI Agent** — 14 ngā taputapu, 3 ngā kaiwhakarato (GPT/Gemini/Claude), streaming, tātaritanga whakaahua
3. **Tuhinga Kōwae** — Monaco me te whakauru AI, te kitea hapa, te tuku
4. **SFTP Manager** — te whakahaere kōnae tākina me te tākina, te whakatika i te tawhiti
5. **Te Mātai** — ngā ine i te wā, te tātaritanga rārangi, te mapi topologia, te tirohanga haumaru

## Ngā Āhuatanga

### AI Ka Mahi Tūturu i Runga i tō Rorohiko

**AI DevOps Agent** — Tīpakohia `gpt`, `gemini`, rānei `claude` i roto i te terminal. Kei te AI 14 ngā taputapu: pānui/tuhi/whakatika kōnae, whakahaere whakahau, git, grep, te whakahaere i ngā tukanga. Ngā whakautu streaming. Te mahara wāhanga. Kāore e tūtohu noa — ka mahi.

**AI Arena** — Tīpakohia `arena`. 2–4 AI e whakataetae ana i te taha i runga i ngā pākete Git motuhake. Ka tirohia e te pātea te tohu i ia rongoā. Tīhia te toa ka whakakotahi.

**AI Council** — Ka kōrero ngā AI maha mō tō pātai, ka whakakotahi i te whakautu pai.

**AI Tuhinga Kōwae** — Te kaiwhakahaere Monaco me te whakauru AI, te kitea hapa, te tuku kotahi ki te rorohiko.

**AI Self-Healing** — Piki CPU, kī kōpae, ratonga kāore e mahi — ka kitea, ka tātaritia, ka whakatika te AI. 5-level autonomous loop.

**Interface Reo** — Kōrero whakahau, rongo i ngā whakautu. Te whakahaere rorohiko kāore e whakamahi i ngā ringa.

### Whakaurunga Multimodal (Te Tuatahi i te Ao i roto i te CLI)

Whakauru whakaahua, tākina kōnae, tākina ataata — tika ki roto i tō SSH terminal. Ka tātaritia e te AI ngā mea katoa: ngā whakaahua hapa, ngā kōnae whakaritenga, ngā ataata tuku, ngā mapi hanganga.

### Te Mātai Hanganga

**Mapi Topologia** — Tirohia ngā rorohiko, ngā ratonga, me ngā hononga i te tirohanga kotahi. Te awhina aunoa mā SSH — kāore he tāutanga agent.

**Te Mātai i te Wā** — CPU, mahara, kōpae, ngā rārangi sparklines + te matapae AI + te whakahoki aunoa. Ka whakakapi i te Datadog.

**Te Tātaritanga Rārangi AI** — Te streaming i te wā + te kitea i ngā raru + te rapu i te reo natural + te hono i ngā rorohiko. Ka whakakapi i te Splunk.

### Haumaru & DevOps

**Pokanga Haumaru** — Te tirohanga 7-ārea + te tohu haumaru + te whakatika kotahi + te tātaritanga AI + te kūti haumaru i te wā. Ka whakakapi i te Qualys.

**Kaiwhakahaere Docker** — Te whakahaere Docker katoa mā SSH — kāore he agent. Te whakahaere i ngā ipu, ngā rauemi ora, te tātaritanga rārangi AI, te kaiwhakahaere Compose. Ka whakakapi i te Portainer.

**Te Whakautu aunoa i ngā Pukapuka** — Ka whakahaerehia e te AI ngā pukapuka aunoa. Te hanga i te reo natural, te peka tikanga, te whakahoki aunoa. He Pukapuka e ora ana e pākiki ana. Ka whakakapi i te PagerDuty.

**Kaiwhakahaere Cron & Env** — Te kaiwhakahaere crontab whakaahua + te whakahaere kōnae .env. Ka huri te AI i te reo natural ki ngā whakaritenga cron.

### Te Tautoko

**Terminal Multiplayer** — Tīhia ngā wāhanga terminal i te wā e pā ana ki ngā hoa mahi.

**Wāhi Mahi Rōpū & RBAC** — Te whakahaere i ngā mana i runga i ngā tūranga, ngā rārangi tirohanga, te tātaritanga mōrea whakahau.

### Te Whakarite

**59 Ngā Plugins** — Te mapi Docker, te tirohanga Kubernetes, te tirohanga Redis, te kaiwhakahaere PM2, te tirohanga MongoDB, te mātai whatunga, me te nui atu. Te mākete plugin me te tāuta kotahi.

**Kaiwhakamahi API** — Te kaiwhakamahi REST/GraphQL/WebSocket i hangaia. Tīmatahia ngā API kāore e wehe i te terminal.

### Mō te Katoa

**254 Ngā Reo** — Ia reo, ia tuhinga, tautoko RTL. Kāore he pae.

**Te Whakauru Katoa** — Te pānui i te mata (ARIA), te putanga terminal braille, te mōhiti teitei, ngā whakahau reo, te tirohanga ki te papapātuhi, te whakarite i te rahi tuhinga.

## Te Kōrero

I whanau a WIA SOOM mai i te hapa tuhinga me te mata pakaru.

Ko Sam ahau, he kaiwhakamahi rorohiko i ako anake i te 50+. I ako ahau ki te tuhinga i muri i te 50 me te whakapau 2 tau ki te whakahaere i ngā rorohiko i te ara uaua — te hanga i ngā tuhinga pānui 40,000+, 434 ngā taputapu, me ngā ratonga paetukutuku 30, i te pō i muri i te pō.

**Te hapa.** I te marama kua pahure, i whakamahi ahau i tāku SSH terminal ka tuhinga hē `Claude`. I huri te mata — i puta te Claude Code. Kāore au i te mōhio he aha ia. Engari i whakatūria e taua kitenga hē he rākau: me pēhea te hanga i tētahi terminal e noho ana te AI i roto?

**Te hanga tuatahi.** I ako ahau i a au anō i te Electron, i hanga te putanga tuatahi o WIA SOOM, ā, i whakahaerehia. I puta te mata whakauru o Claude Code — he pakaru, he hē, e whiti ana pēnei i te tohu e mate ana. Engari i ora ia.

I tuhinga ahau: **"Ka taea e koe te kōrero?"**

I whakahoki te AI: *"Āe, ka taea e au te kōrero. He aha te āwhina e taea e au te tuku?"*

I muri mai, i pātai ahau: **"Ka taea e koe te pānui i ngā whakaahua?"**

I whakahoki te AI: *"Āe, ka taea e au te pānui i ngā whakaahua! He aha te mea e hiahia ana koe?"*

Nā ngā pātai e rua, i pātaihia i roto i tāku ake taupānga e te tangata kāore e mōhio he aha te "mea kāore e taea" i roto i te terminal CLI, i riro hei terminal SSH tuatahi i te ao me te whakauru whakaahua.

I ngā rā e 7: 67,000+ ngā rārangi waehere. 59 ngā plugins. 254 ngā reo. AI Arena. Ngā agent e whakatika ana i a rātou anō.

Kāore ngā kaiwhakawhanake whai wheako i hanga tēnei — nā te mea i "mōhio" rātou kāore e taea te whakaahua i roto i ngā terminal CLI. Kāore au i te mōhio i taua mea. Nā reira, i pātai noa ahau.
