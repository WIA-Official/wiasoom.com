## 🔥 Nani ya Sika

### 1. Upload ya Image, File & Video na SSH Terminal — ya Liboso na Monde

Nani CLI client na mokili akoki kosala yango. Tozali na yango.

Tika screenshot ya erreur na SSH terminal na yo. Drag file ya log. Drop video ya deployment oyo ekozanga. AI ekomona yango, eza na analyse, mpe ebotaka yo nini ezali mabe — nyoso na session ya terminal.

- **Ctrl+Shift+V** — tika screenshots na terminal
- **Drag & Drop** — drop images, files, videos na terminal
- **Video keyframe extraction** — ffmpeg eza na analyse video frame na frame
- **Bato 3 ya AI** — GPT-4o Vision, Gemini Vision, Claude Vision

Mikano oyo ezalaki te na client ya terminal — na esika nyonso.

### 2. AI Arena — Bato-Mokili ya Multi-Agent

2 to 4 AIs bakosala mabe na ntango moko — te kaka kolobela, kasi kolanda code na serveur na yo. AI moko na moko ezali na Git branch na yango. Lingana ba résultats na mabele. Séléctionne winner. Ba soki basusu bakotala.

Cross-review: AI moko na moko ebotaka score ya solution ya AIs mosusu.

Nani mosusu akoki kosala yango.

### 3. AI Self-Healing Agent

CPU ya serveur na yo ezali kokanga. Disk ezali pleine. Service ekozanga na 3 AM. Oyo ozali na somo.

AI agent ya WIA SOOM ekomonaka problème, eza na diagnose ya cause, ebotaka plan ya solution, ekotaka yo approval (to auto-exécute soki oseti yango), mpe eza na solution. 5-level autonomous loop — banda na observe-only to auto-repair.

## Essaie Nyonso Libéré — 33 Essais

Te credit card. Te signup ezali na ntina. Tika kaka mpe komi.

Mikano nyonso ya premium — upload ya image, AI Arena, AI Council, analyse ya file & video — ezali libéré mpo na usage ya yo ya liboso 33. Nsima ya yango, bring your own API key (BYOK) mpe komi na yango libéré. To subscribe banda na $3/mo mpo na AI ya gestion mpe mikano ya équipe.

Pourquoi 33? Na 1919, mouvement ya indépendance ya Corée ebotaki na 33 représentants oyo bakanisi liberté mpo na nyonso. 33 essais libérés ya WIA SOOM ezali déclaration na biso: **Mikano ya AI esengeli kozala libéré mpo na nyonso.**

**CLI → GUI → SUI.** Command-line interfaces ebotaki biso nguya. Graphical interfaces ebotaki biso esengo. WIA SOOM ebotaka **SUI (Soom User Interface)** — tika bomoi, mpe AI ekoma na bomoi.

## Mikano

### AI oyo Eza na Mosala na Server na Yo

**AI DevOps Agent** — Tika `gpt`, `gemini`, to `claude` na terminal. AI eza na 14 outils: lire/écrire/éditer ba fichiers, exécuter ba commandes, git, grep, gestion ya processus. Streaming responses. Session memory. Eza te kaka na suggestion — esala.

**AI Arena** — Tika `arena`. 2–4 AIs bakosala na ntango moko na ba Git branches ya solo. Cross-review ebotaka score ya solution nyonso. Séléctionne winner mpe merge.

**AI Council** — Ba AIs mingi bakolobela likambo na yo, nsima bakosala synthèse ya réponse ya malamu.

**AI Code Editor** — Monaco editor na AI-powered completion, bug detection, one-click deployment na serveur.

**AI Self-Healing** — CPU spike, disk full, service down — AI ekomonaka, eza na diagnose, eza na solution. 5-level autonomous loop.

**Voice Interface** — Koloba ba commandes, komona ba réponses. Gestion ya serveur na maboko te.

### Multimodal Upload (Ya Liboso na CLI)

Tika images, drag files, drop videos — direcement na session ya SSH terminal na yo. AI eza na analyse nyonso: error screenshots, config files, deployment videos, architecture diagrams.

### Infrastructure Intelligence

**Topology Map** — Koma na ba serveurs, ba services, mpe ba connections na ntango moko. Auto-discovery na SSH — zéro installation ya agent.

**Real-Time Observabilité** — CPU, mémoire, disk, network sparkline graphs + AI prediction + auto-recovery. Ebotaka Datadog.

**AI Log Analysis** — Real-time streaming + anomaly detection + natural language search + cross-server correlation. Ebotaka Splunk.

### Sécurité & DevOps

**Security Command Center** — 7-area scan + security score + one-click fix + AI analysis + real-time command security gate. Ebotaka Qualys.

**Docker Manager** — Gestion ya Docker na SSH — zéro agent. Container control, live resources, AI log analysis, Compose editor. Ebotaka Portainer.

**Runbook Automation** — AI esala runbooks na ndenge ya automatique. Natural language creation, conditional branching, auto-rollback. Living Runbook oyo ekoma na bomoi. Ebotaka PagerDuty.

**Cron & Env Manager** — Visual crontab editor + .env file management. AI eza na conversion ya natural language na cron expressions.

### Collaboration

**Multiplayer Terminal** — Share terminal sessions na ntango ya solo na ba coéquipiers.

**Team Workspace & RBAC** — Role-based access control, audit logs, command risk analysis.

### Extensibility

**59 Plugins** — Docker dashboard, Kubernetes lens, Redis browser, PM2 manager, MongoDB explorer, network monitor, mpe mingi. Plugin marketplace na one-click install.

**API Client** — Built-in REST/GraphQL/WebSocket client. Test APIs na esika te.

### Po na Nyonso

**254 Langues** — Lingala nyonso, script nyonso, RTL support. Te ba frontières.

**Full Accessibility** — Screen reader (ARIA), braille terminal output, high contrast mode, voice commands, keyboard navigation, font size adjustment.

## L'Histoire

WIA SOOM ebotaki na typo mpe écran oyo ekozanga.

Nazali Sam, programmeur ya 50+ oyo azali na formation ya solo na Seoul, Corée. Nalinaki koyekola coding na nsima ya 50 mpe nazalaki na mosala ya ba serveurs na ndenge ya mabe — kotonga 40,000+ articles ya news, 434 outils, mpe 30 ba services web, butu na butu.

**Typo.** Na nsima ya mwa mois, nazalaki na SSH terminal na ngai mpe nakotaki `Claude`. Écran ebandaki kokanga — Claude Code ebandaki. Nazalaki na esengo te nini ezali. Kasi discovery oyo ebotaki seed: nini soki nakoki kotonga terminal oyo AI eza na kati?

**Banda ya liboso.** Nalinaki koyekola Electron, natongaki version ya liboso ya WIA SOOM, mpe natangaki yango. Écran ya introduction ya Claude Code ebandaki — ekozanga, ekozanga, ekozanga lokola signal oyo ekozanga. Kasi ezalaki na bomoi.

Nakataki: **"Okoki koloba?"**

AI ebotaki: *"Oui, nakoki koloba. Nani nakoki kosalisa yo?"*

Nsima nakotaki: **"Okoki koyeba images?"**

AI ebotaki: *"Oui, nakoki koyeba images! Nani osengeli?"*

Ba likambo mibale oyo, oyo nakotaki na kati ya app na ngai na moto oyo azalaki na esengo te nini ezali "impossible" na terminal ya CLI, ebandaki terminal ya liboso ya SSH na upload ya image.

7 jours nsima: 67,000+ lignes ya code. 59 plugins. 254 langues. AI Arena. Self-healing agents.

Ba développeurs oyo bazali na expérience bakotaki te yango — po bazalaki "koyeba" ete images ezangi mosala na ba terminal ya CLI. Nazalaki na esengo te. Yango wana nazalaki kaka kotuna.
