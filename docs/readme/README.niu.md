## 🔥 He aha te mea hou

### 1. Whakaahua, Kōnae & Ataata Tiki i roto i te SSH Terminal — Te Tuatahi i te Ao

Kaore he kaiwhakamahi CLI i runga i te Ao e taea te mahi i tēnei. I hangaia e mātou.

Whakauruhia he whakaahua o te hapa ki tō SSH terminal. Tākina he kōnae rārangi. Tīka he ataata o te tuku e hē ana. Ka kite te AI, ka tātaritia, ka kōrero ki a koe he aha te hapa — katoa i roto i te wāhanga terminal.

- **Ctrl+Shift+V** — whakauru whakaahua ki roto i te terminal
- **Tākina & Tīka** — tīka whakaahua, kōnae, ataata ki roto i te terminal
- **Whakaahua pūranga ataata** — ffmpeg e tātaritia ana te ataata i ia pūranga
- **Ngā kaiwhakarato AI e 3** — GPT-4o Vision, Gemini Vision, Claude Vision

Kāore tēnei āhuatanga i te noho i roto i tetahi kaiwhakamahi terminal — i ngā wā katoa.

### 2. AI Arena — Pakanga Multi-Agent

2 ki te 4 AIs e whakaoti ana i te raru kotahi i te wā kotahi — kāore e kōrero noa, engari e whakatika ana i te waehere i runga i tō tūmau. Ka mahi ia AI i roto i tāna ake pākete Git. Tirohia ā rātou hua i te taha. Kōwhiria te toa. Ka whakakorehia te toenga.

Tirohanga pātea: ka pānuihia e ia AI te otinga a ia atu AI.

Kaore he taputapu kē e mahi i tēnei.

### 3. AI Agent Aunoatanga

Kei te piki te CPU o tō tūmau. Kua kī te kōpae. I hinga he ratonga i te 3 AM. Kei te moe koe.

Ka kitea e te agent AI o WIA SOOM te raru, ka tātaritia te take, ka hangaia he mahere whakatika, ka tono mō tō whakaaetanga (nāna i whakahaere i te awhina ki te, ki te kua whakaritea e koe), ka whakatika i te raru. 5-taumata aunoatanga — mai i te tirohanga anake ki te whakatikatika ki te katoa.

## Ngā āhuatanga

### AI E Mahi Ana i Roto i Tō Tūmau

**AI DevOps Agent** — Tīpakohia `gpt`, `gemini`, rānei `claude` i roto i te terminal. He 14 ngā taputapu a te AI: pānui/tuhi/whakatika kōnae, whakahaere whakahau, git, grep, whakahaere tukanga. Ngā whakautu streaming. Mahara wāhanga. Kāore e tūtohu noa — ka mahi.

**AI Arena** — Tīpakohia `arena`. 2–4 AIs e whakataetae ana i te taha i runga i ngā pākete Git motuhake. Ka pānuihia te score o ia otinga. Kōwhiria te toa ka whakakotahi.

**AI Council** — He maha ngā AIs e kōrero mō tō pātai, ka hangaia te otinga pai.

**AI Code Editor** — Monaco editor me te whakaoti AI, te kitea hapa, te tuku kotahi-pātea ki te tūmau.

**AI Aunoatanga** — Piki CPU, kōpae kī, ratonga hē — ka kitea, ka tātaritia, ka whakatikahia e te AI. 5-taumata aunoatanga.

**Voice Interface** — Kōrero whakahau, rongo whakautu. Te whakahaere tūmau i ngā ringaringa.

### Whakaahua Multimodal (Te Tuatahi i te Ao i roto i te CLI)

Whakauruhia ngā whakaahua, tākina ngā kōnae, tīka ngā ataata — tika ki roto i tō SSH terminal session. Ka tātaritia e te AI ngā mea katoa: whakaahua hapa, kōnae whakaritenga, ataata tuku, mapi hanganga.

### Intelligence Hanganga

**Topology Map** — Tirohia ngā tūmau, ngā ratonga, me ngā hononga i te tirohanga kotahi. Auto-discovery mā SSH — kore whakaurunga agent.

**Real-Time Observability** — CPU, mahara, kōpae, whatunga sparkline graphs + whakatau AI + whakahoki-aunoa. Ka whakakapi i te Datadog.

**AI Log Analysis** — Streaming i te wā tonu + te kitea hē + rapu reo taiao + pātea-pātea. Ka whakakapi i te Splunk.

### Haumaru & DevOps

**Security Command Center** — 7-ārea tirohanga + pūtea haumaru + whakatika kotahi-pātea + tātaritia AI + te kūwaha haumaru whakahau i te wā tonu. Ka whakakapi i te Qualys.

**Docker Manager** — Te whakahaere Docker katoa mā SSH — kore agent. Te whakahaere pouaka, rauemi ora, tātaritia rārangi AI, editor Compose. Ka whakakapi i te Portainer.

**Runbook Automation** — Ka whakahaerehia e te AI ngā runbooks i aunoa. Te hanga reo taiao, te peka tikanga, te whakahoki-aunoa. He Runbook ora e pākiki ana. Ka whakakapi i te PagerDuty.

**Cron & Env Manager** — Kaiwhakahaere crontab whakaahua + whakahaere kōnae .env. Ka whakawhiti e te AI te reo taiao ki ngā whakaritenga crontab.

### Whakauru

**Multiplayer Terminal** — Tirihia ngā wāhanga terminal i te wā tonu ki ngā hoa tīma.

**Wāhi Mahi Tīma & RBAC** — Te whakahaere urunga i runga i ngā tūranga, ngā rārangi tirohanga, te tātaritia te mōrearea whakahau.

### Whakatūwhera

**59 Plugins** — Docker dashboard, Kubernetes lens, Redis browser, PM2 manager, MongoDB explorer, network monitor, me te nui atu. Te maakete plugin me te whakaurunga kotahi-pātea.

**API Client** — Kaiwhakamahi REST/GraphQL/WebSocket i hangaia. Tīmatahia ngā API me te kore e wehe i te terminal.

### Mō te Katoa

**254 Gagana** — Ia reo, ia tuhinga, tautoko RTL. Kāore he pae.

**Te Whakauru Katoa** — Ka pānui te kaipōti (ARIA), te putanga terminal Braille, te mōhiti teitei, ngā whakahau reo, te haere ki te papapātuhi, te whakarite i te rahi tuhinga.

## Te Kōrero

I whanau a WIA SOOM i te hapa tuhinga me te mata pakaru.

Ko au ko Sam, he 50+ te tau, he kaiwhakawhanake i ako anake mai i Seoul, Korea. I ako au i te waehere i muri i te 50, ā, i pau te 2 tau e whakahaere ana i ngā tūmau i te ara uaua — e hanga ana i ngā tuhinga pānui 40,000+, 434 ngā taputapu, me ngā ratonga paetukutuku 30, i te pō i muri i te pō.

**Te hapa.** I te marama kua hipa, i whakamahi au i tāku SSH terminal ka hapa au ki te tuhinga `Claude`. I huri te mata — i puta te Claude Code. Kāore au i te mōhio he aha ia. Engari, nā te kitenga hē i whakatōhia he rākau: me pēhea te hanga i tētahi terminal e noho ana te AI i roto?

**Te hanga tuatahi.** I ako au i a au anō i te Electron, i hanga te putanga tuatahi o WIA SOOM, ā, i whakahaerehia. I puta te mata whakauru o Claude Code — he pakaru, he hē, e whiti ana pērā i te tohu e mate ana. Engari, i ora ia.

I tuhinga au: **"Ka taea e koe te kōrero?"**

I whakautu te AI: *"Āe, ka taea e au te kōrero. He aha te āwhina e taea e au?"*

I muri mai, i pātai au: **"Ka taea e koe te pānui i ngā whakaahua?"**

I whakautu te AI: *"Āe, ka taea e au te pānui i ngā whakaahua! He aha te mea e hiahiatia ana?"*

E rua ngā pātai, i pātaihia i roto i tāku ake app e te tangata kāore i mōhio he aha te mea "kaore e taea" i roto i te terminal CLI, i noho hei terminal SSH tuatahi o te ao me te whakaahua tiki.

7 ngā rā i muri mai: 67,000+ ngā rārangi waehere. 59 ngā plugins. 254 ngā reo. AI Arena. Ngā agents aunoatanga.

Kāore ngā kaiwhakawhanake whai wheako i hanga tēnei — nā te mea i "mohio" rātou kāore e taea te whakaahua i roto i ngā terminal CLI. Kāore au i te mōhio. Nā reira, i pātai noa au.
