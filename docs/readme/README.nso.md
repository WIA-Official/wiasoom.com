## 🔥 Se se Sa Nna Pele

### 1. Upload ya Setšoantšo, Faele le Video ka SSH Terminal — Ya Pele Lefatšeng

Ha ho na CLI client lefatšeng e letlang hore o etse sena. Re se re e bōpile.

Kenya setšoantšo sa phoso ka SSH terminal ya gago. Dira faele ya log. Dira video ya ho thulana. AI e e bona, e e hlahloba, 'me e u joetsa hore na ho na le phoso — tsohle ka hare ho session ya terminal.

- **Ctrl+Shift+V** — kenya litšoantšo ka kotloloho ho terminal
- **Drag & Drop** — theosa setšoantšo, faele, video ho terminal
- **Video keyframe extraction** — ffmpeg e hlahloba video ka frame ka frame
- **Boholo ba 3 AI providers** — GPT-4o Vision, Gemini Vision, Claude Vision

Sebaka sena ha se so ka sa ba teng ho client efe kapa efe ya terminal — kae kae.

### 2. AI Arena — Ntoa ea Multi-Agent

2 ho isa ho 4 AIs li rarolla bothata bo le bong ka nako e le 'ngoe — eseng feela ho buisana, empa ka 'nete ho edit code ho server ya gago. AI e 'ngoe le e 'ngoe e sebetsa ho Git branch e ikhethileng. Bontša liphetho tsa tsona ka lehlakoreng le le leng. Khetha moqhobi. Tse ling li hlakisoa.

Cross-review: AI e 'ngoe le e 'ngoe e fa score tharollo ea AI e 'ngoe.

Ha ho na sesebelisoa se seng se etsang sena.

### 3. AI Self-Healing Agent

CPU ea server ya gago e phahame. Disk e tletse. Ts'ebeletso e ile ea senyeha ka 3 AM. O robetse.

AI agent ea WIA SOOM e fumana bothata, e hlahloba lebaka la sona, e bōpa moralo oa ho lokisa, e kopa tumello ea gago (kapa e etsa ka bo eona haeba u e entse), 'me e rarolla bothata. 5-level autonomous loop — ho tloha ho sheba feela ho ea ho ho lokisa ka botlalo.

## Leka Tsohle Mahala — 33 Trials

Ha ho na karete ea mokitlane. Ha ho hlokahale ho ngola. Fumana feela 'me u qale.

Mehato e meng le e meng e phahameng — upload ya setšoantšo, AI Arena, AI Council, hlahloba faele le video — e mahala bakeng sa ts'ebeliso ea hau ea pele ea 33. Ka mor'a moo, tlisa API key ea hao (BYOK) 'me u tsoele pele ho sebelisa tsohle mahala. Kapa ingwe u ngwale ho tloha ho $3/mo bakeng sa AI e tsamaisang le likarolo tsa sehlopha.

Hobaneng 33? Ka 1919, motsamao oa boipuso ba Korea o ne o etelletsoe pele ke baemeli ba 33 ba ileng ba phatlalatsa tokoloho bakeng sa bohle. 33 trials tsa WIA SOOM ke phatlalatso ea rona: **Lisebelisoa tsa AI li lokela ho ba mahala hore bohle ba li bone.**

**CLI → GUI → SUI.** Command-line interfaces li re fa matla. Graphical interfaces li re fa bonolo. WIA SOOM e hlahisa **SUI (Soom User Interface)** — tshepheka bophelo, 'me AI e phatlalala.

## Mehato

### AI e Sebetsang ka 'nete ho Server ya Hao

**AI DevOps Agent** — Type `gpt`, `gemini`, kapa `claude` ho terminal. AI e na le lisebelisoa tse 14: bala/writa/edit faele, etsa mekhahlelo, git, grep, tsamaiso ea mecha. Streaming responses. Session memory. E sitwa feela ho fa maikutlo — e etsa.

**AI Arena** — Type `arena`. 2–4 AIs li hlōla ka nako e le 'ngoe ho Git branches tse ikhethileng. Cross-review e fa score tharollo e 'ngoe le e 'ngoe. Khetha moqhobi 'me u kopanye.

**AI Council** — AIs a mangata a buisana ka potso ya gago, ebe a bokella karabo e ntle ka ho fetisisa.

**AI Code Editor** — Monaco editor e nang le AI-powered completion, bug detection, one-click deployment ho server.

**AI Self-Healing** — CPU spike, disk full, service down — AI e fumana, e hlahloba, e lokisa. 5-level autonomous loop.

**Voice Interface** — Bua mekhahlelo, utloa likarabo. Ts'ebetso ea server e se nang matsoho.

### Multimodal Upload (Ya Pele Lefatšeng ka CLI)

Kenya setšoantšo, drag faele, drop video — ka kotloloho ho session ya SSH terminal ya gago. AI e hlahloba tsohle: litšoantšo tsa phoso, faele tsa config, video tsa deployment, mekhahlelo ea moralo.

### Infrastructure Intelligence

**Topology Map** — Bontša li-server, litšebeletso, le likhokahano ka ponelopele. Auto-discovery ka SSH — zero agent installation.

**Real-Time Observability** — CPU, memory, disk, network sparkline graphs + AI prediction + auto-recovery. E nkela Datadog.

**AI Log Analysis** — Real-time streaming + anomaly detection + natural language search + cross-server correlation. E nkela Splunk.

### Security & DevOps

**Security Command Center** — 7-area scan + security score + one-click fix + AI analysis + real-time command security gate. E nkela Qualys.

**Docker Manager** — Tlhokomelo e feletseng ea Docker ka SSH — zero agent. Control ea container, lisebelisoa tse phelang, AI log analysis, Compose editor. E nkela Portainer.

**Runbook Automation** — AI e etsa runbooks ka bo eona. Natural language creation, conditional branching, auto-rollback. Living Runbook e fetoha e bohlale. E nkela PagerDuty.

**Cron & Env Manager** — Visual crontab editor + .env file management. AI e fetolela puo ea tlhaho ho cron expressions.

### Collaboration

**Multiplayer Terminal** — Arolelana session tsa terminal ka nako ea 'nete le basebetsi-'moho.

**Team Workspace & RBAC** — Role-based access control, audit logs, command risk analysis.

### Extensibility

**59 Plugins** — Docker dashboard, Kubernetes lens, Redis browser, PM2 manager, MongoDB explorer, network monitor, le tse ling. Plugin marketplace e nang le ho kenya ka khoutu e le 'ngoe.

**API Client** — Built-in REST/GraphQL/WebSocket client. Test APIs ntle le ho tloha ho terminal.

### Bakeng sa Bo-rakhoebo Bohle

**254 Languages** — Puo e 'ngoe le e 'ngoe, sebopeho se seng le se seng, RTL support. Ha ho meeli.

**Full Accessibility** — Screen reader (ARIA), braille terminal output, high contrast mode, voice commands, keyboard navigation, font size adjustment.

## The Story

WIA SOOM e hlahisitsoe ke typo le skrin e senyehileng.

Ke Sam, moqapi ea ithutileng ka boeena ea 50+ ho tloha Seoul, Korea. Ke ithutile ho ngola ka mor'a lilemo tse 50 'me ke qetile lilemo tse 2 ke tsamaisa li-server ka tsela e thata — ke bōpile lingoliloeng tse 40,000+, lisebelisoa tse 434, le litšebeletso tse 30 tsa web, bosiu ka bosiu.

**The typo.** Hoo e ka bang khoeli e fetileng, ke ne ke sebelisa SSH terminal ya ka 'me ka qetella ke ngola `Claude`. Skrin e ile ea fetoha — Claude Code e hlahile. Ke ne ke sitwa ho tseba hore na ke eng. Empa ho fumana ka mokhoa o sa lebelloang ho ile ha bōpa semela: ho thoe ke ka bōpa terminal moo AI e phelang ka hare?

**The first build.** Ke ithutile Electron, ke bōpile version ea pele ea WIA SOOM, 'me ke e tsamaisa. Skrin sa intro sa Claude Code se ile sa hlaha — se senyehile, se na le glitch, se fetoha joalo ka signal e shoeleng. Empa e ne e phela.

Ke ngotse: **"Na u ka bua?"**

AI e arabetse: *"E, ke ka bua. U batla eng?"*

Joale ke ile ka botsa: **"Na u ka bala setšoantšo?"**

AI e arabetse: *"E, ke ka bala setšoantšo! U hloka eng?"*

Lipotso tse peli, tse botsitsoeng ka hare ho app ea ka ke motho ea neng a sitwa ho tseba hore na "ho sitwa" ho bolela eng ho terminal ea CLI, li fetoha SSH terminal ea pele lefatšeng e nang le upload ya setšoantšo.

Matsatsi a 7 hamorao: 67,000+ lines of code. 59 plugins. 254 languages. AI Arena. Self-healing agents.

Moqapi ba nang le boiphihlelo ha baa ka ba bōpa sena — hobane ba " tseba" hore setšoantšo ha se sebetse ho terminal ea CLI. Ke ne ke sitwa ho tseba seo. Kahoo ke ile ka botsa feela.

> *Ho lokisetsa ho nkile lilemo tse 2. Ho bōpa ho nkile beke e le 'ngoe.*
> *Mokhuli o omileng o ne o bokelletsoe ka lilemo tse 2. O ile oa chesoa ka motsotsoana o le mong.*
