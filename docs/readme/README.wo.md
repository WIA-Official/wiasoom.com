## 🔥 Lii am nañu

### 1. Jàpp ci SSH Terminal — Lii am nañu ci àdduna

Naka CLI client ci àdduna mu jox la. Nuy jàpp.

Jottali ci screenshot bu amee xew ci sa SSH terminal. Jàppal ci log file. Jottali ci vidéo bu amee xew. AI dafa jàpp, dafa jàppal, te dafa jox la li amul xew — bu baax ci terminal session bi.

- **Ctrl+Shift+V** — jottali screenshots ci terminal
- **Jàpp & Jottali** — jottali images, files, vidéos ci terminal
- **Video keyframe extraction** — ffmpeg dafa jàppal vidéo frame ci frame
- **Benn 3 AI providers** — GPT-4o Vision, Gemini Vision, Claude Vision

Lii amul ci tool bu amul ci terminal client — ci biir.

### 2. AI Arena — Multi-Agent Battle

2 ci 4 AIs jàppal lii am nañu ci jamono bu nekk — te duñu jàppal, waaye duñu jàppal ci code ci sa server. Kàddu kàddu AI jàpp ci Git branch bu am solo. Jàppal lii am nañu ci jàmm. Jàppal lii am nañu ci jàmm. Li jàppul, nuy jàpp.

Cross-review: kàddu kàddu AI jàpp ci lii am nañu ci kàddu kàddu AI.

Tool bu amul ci loolu.

### 3. AI Self-Healing Agent

CPU ci sa server dafa xew. Disk dafa dolle. Benn service dafa xew ci 3 AM. Yow dafa jàpp.

AI agent bi WIA SOOM dafa jàpp lii am nañu, dafa jàppal lii am nañu, dafa jàppal plan bu jàmm, dafa jàppal sa jàmm (waaye dafa jàppal auto-execute bu yow jàppal), te dafa jàpp lii am nañu. 5-level autonomous loop — ci jàppal ci jàmm ci full auto-repair.

## The Story

WIA SOOM dafa jàpp ci typo ak screen bu xew.

Maa ngi Sam, programmer bu self-taught ci 50+ ci Seoul, Korea. Maa ngi jàppal coding ci 50 ak jàppal 2 at ci server yi ci loolu — jàppal 40,000+ articles, 434 tools, ak 30 web services, bés bu nekk.

**Lii typo.** Ci at bu jàmm, maa ngi jàppal ci sa SSH terminal ak jàppal `Claude`. Screen bi dafa jàpp — Claude Code dafa am. Dama amul lii. Waaye loolu dafa jàppal seed: naka bu jàppal ci terminal bu am AI ci biir?

**Lii first build.** Maa ngi jàppal Electron, jàppal version bu jàmm bi WIA SOOM, ak jàppal loolu. Claude Code's intro screen dafa jàpp — broken, glitched, flickering sama xew. Waaye dafa am.

Maa ngi jàpp: **"Ndax danga jàppal?"**

AI dafa jàpp: *"Waaw, danga jàppal. Naka la ma jàpp?"*

Boo jàppal: **"Ndax danga jàpp images?"**

AI dafa jàpp: *"Waaw, danga jàpp images! Naka la ma jàpp?"*

Lii ñaari suqali, jàppal ci sama app bu amul lii "impossible" ci terminal CLI, dafa jàppal loolu ci SSH terminal bu am image upload.

7 jours ci kanam: 67,000+ lines of code. 59 plugins. 254 languages. AI Arena. Self-healing agents.

Developers yi am nañu loolu duñu jàppal loolu — ndax am nañu "xew" images duñu jàpp ci CLI terminals. Dama amul loolu. Waaye maa ngi jàppal.
