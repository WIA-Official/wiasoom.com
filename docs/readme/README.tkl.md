## 🔥 He aha te mea kāore i mahia i mua

### 1. Whakauru Whakaahua, Kōnae & Ataata i roto i te SSH Terminal — Te Ao Tuatahi

Kāore he kaiwhakamahi CLI i runga i te Ao e tuku i tēnei. I hangaia e mātou.

Tārua he whakaahua o te hapa ki tō SSH terminal. Tīka te kōnae rārangi. Tīka he ataata o te tuku kāore e angitu. Ka kite te AI, ka tātaritia, ka kī mai ki a koe he aha te hapa — katoa i roto i te wāhanga terminal.

- **Ctrl+Shift+V** — tārua whakaahua ki roto i te terminal
- **Tīka & Tīka** — tīka whakaahua, kōnae, ataata ki roto i te terminal
- **Whakaahua kāinga ataata** — ffmpeg ka tātaritia te ataata i te whāinga
- **Ngā kaiwhakarato AI e toru** — GPT-4o Vision, Gemini Vision, Claude Vision

Kāore tēnei āhuatanga i kitea i roto i tetahi kaiwhakamahi terminal — i ngā wā katoa.

### 2. AI Arena — Pakanga Multi-Agent

2 ki te 4 AIs e whakaoti ana i te raru kotahi i te wā kotahi — kāore e kōrero noa, engari e whakatika ana i te waehere i runga i tō tūmau. Ka mahi ia AI i roto i tāna ake peka Git motuhake. Tirohia ō rātou hua i te taha. Tīhia te toa. Ka whakakorehia te toenga.

Cross-review: ka tāpaea e ia AI te tohu i ia rongoā a te AI.

Kāore he taputapu kē e mahi i tēnei.

### 3. AI Self-Healing Agent

Kei te piki te CPU o tō tūmau. Kua kī te kōpae. I pakaru he ratonga i te 3 AM. E moe ana koe.

Ka kitea e te agent AI o WIA SOOM te raru, ka tātaritia te take, ka hangaia he mahere whakatika, ka tono mō tō whakaaetanga (rānei ka whakahaerehia aunoa mēnā kua whakaritea e koe), ka whakatika i te raru. 5-taumata huringa motuhake — mai i te tirohanga anake ki te whakatikatika aunoa.

**CLI → GUI → SUI.** Nā te interfacē rārangi whakahau i tuku mai te mana ki a mātou. Nā te interfacē rārangi whakaahua i tuku mai te māmā. Ka whakauru a WIA SOOM i te **SUI (Soom User Interface)** — tōku ora, ka ora te AI.

## 5-i-1 Pātai

Ka mahi ngā taputapu kē i te mea kotahi. Ka mahi a WIA SOOM i te rima — i roto i te kotahi matapihi.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ WIA SOOM                                              │
├──────┬──────┬──────┬──────┬──────────────────────────┤
│  SSH │  AI  │ Code │ SFTP │ Observability             │
│  Ter │ Agen │ Edit │ File │ Tirohanga + Haumaru      │
│  min │   t  │  or  │ Mgr  │ + Rārangi + Topology     │
│  al  │      │      │      │                           │
└──────┴──────┴──────┴──────┴──────────────────────────┘
```

1. **SSH Terminal** — xterm.js, bastion tunneling, split views, multiplayer sharing
2. **AI Agent** — 14 ngā taputapu, 3 ngā kaiwhakarato (GPT/Gemini/Claude), streaming, tātari whakaahua
3. **Code Editor** — Monaco me te whakaoti AI, te kite hapa, te tuku
4. **SFTP Manager** — whakahaere kōnae tīka me te tuku i te tawhiti
5. **Observability** — ngā pānga i te wā tonu, tātari rārangi, mapi topological, tirohanga haumaru

## Ngā Āhuatanga

### AI E Mahi Ana i Roto i Tō Tūmau

**AI DevOps Agent** — Tīpia `gpt`, `gemini`, rānei `claude` ki te terminal. He 14 ngā taputapu tāpiri: pānui/tuhi/whakatika kōnae, whakahaere whakahau, git, grep, whakahaere tukanga. Ka whakautu i runga i te streaming. Mahara wāhanga. Kāore e tūtohu noa — ka mahi.

**AI Arena** — Tīpia `arena`. 2–4 AIs e whakataetae ana i te taha i runga i ngā peka Git motuhake. Ka tāpaea e te cross-review te tohu i ia rongoā. Tīhia te toa ka whakakotahi.

**AI Council** — E kōrero ana ngā AIs maha mō tō pātai, ka hanga i te whakautu pai.

**AI Code Editor** — Monaco editor me te whakaoti AI, te kite hapa, te tuku kotahi ki te tūmau.

**AI Self-Healing** — Piki CPU, kōpae kī, ratonga kāore e mahi — ka kite te AI, ka tātaritia, ka whakatika. 5-taumata huringa motuhake.

**Voice Interface** — Kōrero whakahau, rongo i ngā whakautu. Te whakahaere i te tūmau me ngā ringaringa kore.

### Whakauru Multimodal (Te Ao Tuatahi i roto i te CLI)

Tārua whakaahua, tīka kōnae, tīka ataata — tika ki roto i tō SSH terminal. Ka tātaritia e te AI ngā mea katoa: whakaahua hapa, kōnae whakaritenga, ataata tuku, mapi hanganga.

### Te Mātauranga Hanganga

**Topology Map** — Tirohia ngā tūmau, ngā ratonga, me ngā hononga i te tirohanga kotahi. Te auto-discovery mā SSH — kāore he whakauru agent.

**Real-Time Observability** — CPU, mahara, kōpae, rārangi tīranga + whakatau AI + te whakahoki aunoa. Ka whakakapi i te Datadog.

**AI Log Analysis** — Streaming i te wā tonu + te kite i ngā raru + te rapu i te reo natural + te whakawhitiwhiti i ngā tūmau. Ka whakakapi i te Splunk.

### Haumaru & DevOps

**Pokapū Whakahaere Haumaru** — tirohanga 7-ārea + tohu haumaru + whakatika kotahi-tārua + tātari AI + te kūti haumaru i te wā tonu. Ka whakakapi i te Qualys.

**Kaiwhakahaere Docker** — Te whakahaere Docker katoa mā SSH — kāore he agent. Te whakahaere i ngā ipu, ngā rauemi ora, tātari rārangi AI, kaiwhakahaere Compose. Ka whakakapi i te Portainer.

**Aunoa te Whakahaere Pukapuka** — Ka whakahaerehia e te AI ngā pukapuka aunoa. Te hanga i te reo natural, te peka tikanga, te hokinga aunoa. He Pukapuka e ora ana e pākiki ana. Ka whakakapi i te PagerDuty.

**Kaiwhakahaere Cron & Env** — Kaiwhakahaere crontab whakaahua + te whakahaere kōnae .env. Ka huri te AI i te reo natural ki ngā whakaritenga cron.

### Te Whakawhanaungatanga

**Multiplayer Terminal** — Tīhia ngā wāhanga terminal i te wā tonu me ngā hoa rōpū.

**Te Wāhi Mahi Rōpū & RBAC** — Te whakahaere i te mana i runga i ngā tūranga, ngā rārangi tirotiro, te tātari i te mōrea whakahau.

### Te Whakaritea

**59 Plugins** — Dashboard Docker, Kubernetes lens, Redis browser, PM2 manager, MongoDB explorer, monitor whatunga, me te nui atu. Te mākete plugin me te whakauru kotahi-tārua.

**API Client** — Kaiwhakamahi REST/GraphQL/WebSocket i hangaia. Tīmatahia ngā API kāore e wehe atu i te terminal.

### Mō te Katoa

**254 Ngā Reo** — Ia reo, ia tuhinga, tautoko RTL. Kāore he pae.

**Te Whakauru Katoa** — Te pānui i te mata (ARIA), te putanga terminal braille, te mōhiti teitei, ngā whakahau reo, te huri i ngā pātea, te whakatika i te rahi tuhinga.

## Te Kōrero

I whanau a WIA SOOM mai i te hapa tuhinga me te mata pakaru.

Ko Sam ahau, he 50+ te tau, he kaiwhakawhanake i ako i ahau i Seoul, Korea. I ako au i te waehere i muri i te 50, ā, i pau te 2 tau e whakahaere ana i ngā tūmau i te ara uaua — e hanga ana i ngā tuhinga pānui 40,000+, 434 ngā taputapu, me ngā ratonga paetukutuku 30, i te pō i muri i te pō.

**Te hapa.** I te marama kua hipa, i whakamahi au i tāku SSH terminal ka hapa au i te tuhinga `Claude`. I huri te mata — i puta te Claude Code. Kāore au i te mōhio he aha ia. Engari, i whakatūria e taua kitenga hē he kākano: mehemea ka taea e au te hanga i te terminal e noho ana te AI i roto?

**Te hanga tuatahi.** I ako au i ahau anō i te Electron, i hanga te putanga tuatahi o WIA SOOM, ā, i whakahaerehia. I puta te mata whakauru o Claude Code — he pakaru, he hē, he tārere pērā i te tohu e mate ana. Engari, i ora ia.

I tīpako au: **"Ka taea e koe te kōrero?"**

I whakautu te AI: *"Āe, ka taea e au te kōrero. He aha te āwhina ka taea e au te tuku?"*

I muri mai, i pātai au: **"Ka taea e koe te pānui i ngā whakaahua?"**

I whakautu te AI: *"Āe, ka taea e au te pānui i ngā whakaahua! He aha te hiahia?"*

Nā ēnei pātai e rua, i pātaihia i roto i tāku ake taupānga e te tangata kāore e mōhio he aha te mea "kaore e taea" i roto i te terminal CLI, i noho hei terminal SSH tuatahi o te ao me te whakauru whakaahua.

I te 7 rā i muri mai: 67,000+ ngā rārangi waehere. 59 ngā plugins. 254 ngā reo. AI Arena. Ngā agent e ora ana.

Kāore ngā kaihanga whai wheako i hanga tēnei — nā te mea i "mohio" rātou kāore e taea ngā whakaahua i roto i ngā terminal CLI. Kāore au i te mōhio. Nā reira, i pātai noa au.
